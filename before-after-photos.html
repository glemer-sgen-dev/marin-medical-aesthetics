The standard

What a published pair has to satisfy

Every before and after pair that ever appears here has to meet all of the following. This is the permanent standard, not a temporary position while the page is empty:

  • First-party only. The photographs are of actual patients of this practice, treated at this practice. Nothing from a device manufacturer, a stock library or another clinic.
  • Comparable conditions. The same view, the same pose, and the same lighting on both sides. A pair that flatters through a change of angle or light is not evidence of anything.
  • A documented release on file for each patient, for each pair.
  • Otherwise, no image. If any of the above is missing, the pair does not publish. There is no version of this page that fills the space with something approximate.

Required alongside

What appears next to every pair

Two statements are required by California law and are rendered next to any pair, legibly, rather than buried in a footer:

  • A model disclosure, wherever the person shown is anyone other than an actual patient of this practice who underwent the advertised procedure.
  • "The same results may not occur for all patients."

These are a legal control, not a stylistic preference.

Under California Business and Professions Code section 651, a violation is a misdemeanour and is independently good cause to suspend a physician's licence.

Never

What this page will not do, in any future version of it.

  • No manufacturer, stock or third-party imagery presented as this practice's results.
  • No pair published without the results statement beside it.
  • No non-comparable poses or lighting.
  • No outcome guarantee of any kind.
  • No "typical results" framing, which implies a distribution nobody here has measured.

Where this stands today

Releases are being documented. Until they are on file, this page publishes the standard above and no images. In the meantime, patients' own written words are the proof that does exist, and they are subject to the same rules.
