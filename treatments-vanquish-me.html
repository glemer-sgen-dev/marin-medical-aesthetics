What people come in with

A broad area rather than a small pocket: the abdomen and flanks together, or the thighs, in people close to a stable weight who have a region that has not responded.

The non-contact aspect matters to people who dislike the idea of anything gripping or suctioning the skin.

That second one is a preference rather than a clinical finding, and it is treated as a preference here.

What changes

How treatment here is decided

What you are pointing at gets identified first

Fat, loose skin and fluid can occupy the same area and look similar in a mirror. A licensed MD performs the health evaluation that separates them.

It ends in a written plan you keep

With the series and the review point stated, so you know when it is being reassessed rather than simply continued.

It decides whether a treatment is appropriate at all

Sometimes the answer is that this is the wrong treatment for what you have described.

An evaluation determines whether a treatment is appropriate at all. It is not a safety or outcome guarantee.

What a session is like

You lie under the applicator, which does not touch you. The area warms steadily over the session. It is generally described as comfortable, and people usually return to normal activity immediately. It is a series, spaced over several weeks.

A treatment room in San Anselmo. You lie down; the applicator sits above you.

Who this is for

Who it suits, and what is claimed

This practice's patients are typically adults roughly 35-65 across Marin County who want visible improvement in skin, body contour or pelvic-floor function, and who want a physician evaluating whether a treatment is appropriate for them at all.

No percentage or inch figures, no outcome figures and no before and after photographs are published, because none is this practice's own and verified.

The Medical Board of California requires an appropriate prior examination where prescriptive drugs and devices will be used, and that examination may not be delegated to registered nurses. That is why the evaluation comes first here, stated as the reason the process exists rather than as a safety claim.

The ceiling

What this is not

This treats one region. It makes no claim about your weight.

  • Not a weight-loss treatment. No weight outcome is claimed or implied.
  • Not a treatment for loose skin, which frequently occupies the same area and needs a different approach.
  • Not surgery, and not an alternative to it.
  • Not suitable for everyone. Contraindications are screened at the evaluation.

Starting from the concern replaces shopping a device grid. The treatments index still exists and is still useful; it now sits downstream of the concern rather than ahead of it.
