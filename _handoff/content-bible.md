## §0 — STANDING RULES (these override any brief, any page, any deadline)

### §0.1 — THE BRAKE (constraint F) — the single most important rule in this document

**FDA states, verbatim (D-INCUMBENT-009 / V13, CONFIRMED):** *"These incidents have occurred when counterfeit Botox is injected by licensed and unlicensed individuals and/or in non-medical or unlicensed settings."*

**Therefore: this practice may NEVER claim that physician credentials alone make a treatment safe.** Any "doctor-led, therefore safe" construction — in any tense, voice, headline, subhead, alt text, meta description, schema field, or CTA — is an overclaim that a **federal source directly contradicts**. It is not a matter of tone. It is falsifiable, and the falsifying document is public.

**What IS defensible is the PROCESS claim, and only in this shape:**
> A physician evaluation determines **whether a treatment is appropriate at all**, and what the plan should be. **It is not a safety guarantee or an outcome guarantee.**

The ceiling is not optional garnish — it is part of the claim. Where the process claim is made prominently, its limit ships with it.

**§0.1a — F-BRAKE SUB-RULE (D-OFFER-014).** The practice's own published verbatim contains *"to minimize side effects"*. That is risk-REDUCTION language embedded in a process sentence. It may ship **only inside its complete sentence**. Lifted into a headline and stripped of its process framing, it drifts from process toward an outcome claim and violates §0.1. **Never headline it.**

**§0.1b — The counterfeit-product fact pattern stays OFF the site entirely.** V13 is an INTERNAL authority governing our own claim ceiling. Published on a commercial site — even naming no one — it functions as an industry warning implicating identifiable local operators. That is a UDAP edge with no marketing upside. Do not quote it, paraphrase it, or allude to it in published copy.

### §0.2 — NEVER AUTHOR AN ATTACK LINE (constraint B)

No competitor is **named, characterised, ranked, compared, or alluded to** in any published word. Not on a page, not in a meta description, not in schema, not in an FAQ answer, not in a blog post, not in alt text. The competitive research exists to justify OUR claims internally; **none of it may become site copy.** This includes constructions that name no one but point at everyone: *"unlike other med spas"*, *"most places in Marin"*, *"where nurses work alone"*. Those are attack lines wearing a disguise, and they carry defamation + UDAP exposure.

### §0.3 — NEVER A CLAIM ABOUT A PERSON (constraint C)

Every research finding is about what a **captured document publishes** — never about what a person is, is not, holds, or lacks. **Never** write or imply that any named practitioner "isn't a physician", "isn't board certified", or "has no medical director". A site not publishing a credential is not evidence a person lacks it. `market-intel/belle-marin.md` (D-MARKET-003) is **excluded from all positioning and copy** for exactly this reason and must stay excluded.

### §0.4 — A "NOT FOUND" IS NOT AN ABSENCE (constraint G)

Every `—` cell, "not found", and `0 of 10` figure in `equivalence-table.md` and `positioning-plan.md` means **one thing only**: the term was not found in the 3–7 pages crawled for that competitor. **It is never a finding that a competitor lacks a capability.** No copy may state or imply "we're the only ones who offer X", "no one else in Marin does Y", or "unavailable elsewhere". Write what **we** offer, affirmatively, full stop.

### §0.5 — NEVER UPGRADE A LEGAL POSTURE

Every enforcement matter in `_pain-receipts.md` is an **allegation** — Luther (SDNY) and Canales/Carey (Santa Clara) have **no conviction reported**; two Massachusetts sentencings remain **UNVERIFIED** and are unusable. Copy must never convert an allegation into a conviction, a charge into a finding, or a settlement into an admission. These matters involve private individuals and **must never be named on this site** regardless of posture.

### §0.6 — NO CLAIM SHIPS WITHOUT A RECEIPT

`real_only` is in force (waiver F2, RESOLVED, `real-only` for ALL proof). Never invent a quote, price, statistic, award, report name, year, credential, case, or URL. If a claim has no receipt in this file or the spec, **it does not ship** — it goes to the P7 shopping list as `[OWNER-DATA]`. Representative/illustrative copy is permitted for **non-proof narrative only** and must be visibly marked swappable.

## §4 — Citation tie-break order

When two sources disagree on a fact, resolve **top-down**. The build skill and the P7 falsifier apply this order mechanically.

1. **A binding legal constraint** — B&P § 651 (V21–V25), 16 C.F.R. Part 465 (V26), FDA (V13), and constraints B/C/F/G in §0. *These are not weighed against anything; they win outright and can only ever make a claim narrower.*
