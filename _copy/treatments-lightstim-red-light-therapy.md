## §1 hero (S)

**H1:** LightStim red light therapy in San Anselmo

Low-level light used as an adjunct to other treatments, typically for skin quality and comfort after

## §2 the problem it is aimed at (M)

**Section heading:** What people come in with

Usually not a standalone concern. It is most often added to support recovery after a more substantial
treatment, or used by people who want a gentle, cumulative option with no recovery at all.

## §4 what the treatment involves (S)

**Section heading:** What a session is like

You sit under the light panel for a set period. It is warm and comfortable, there is no recovery, and
you can carry on with your day immediately.

Any benefit is cumulative, so it is used repeatedly rather than as a single event.

## §5 who this is generally for, and what is published about results (S)

**Section heading:** Who it suits, and what is claimed

This practice's patients are typically adults roughly 35-65 across Marin County who want visible
improvement in skin, body contour or pelvic-floor function, and who want a physician evaluating
whether a treatment is appropriate for them at all.

No outcome figures and no before and after photographs are published, because none is on file with a
documented patient release.

## §6 what this does not do (S)

**Section heading:** What this is not

- **Not a treatment for a significant concern on its own.** It supports; it does not resolve.
- **Not a substitute for laser, needling or injectable treatment** where those are what is indicated.
- **Not a medical treatment for any condition**, and no therapeutic claim beyond skin quality and
  post-treatment comfort is made here.
- No outcome guarantee.
