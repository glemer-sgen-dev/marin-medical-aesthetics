## §2 the part that actually wears you down (M)

**Section heading:** The change that follows doing everything right

Loose skin is often what arrives after a success. Weight loss, a fitness change, pregnancy and
recovery all leave skin that no longer fits, and the result can feel like being punished for the
effort.

It is also the concern with the widest gap between what is advertised and what non-surgical treatment
actually delivers.

---

## §3 what a physician evaluation changes (M)

**Section heading:** Why this starts with an evaluation

- **Degree decides everything.** Mild laxity responds reasonably to non-surgical treatment.
  Significant laxity does not, and no number of sessions changes that.
- **The honest ceiling gets stated.** You will be told where your starting point sits relative to what
  the available treatments can do.
- **Cause matters.** Skin loose after major weight change behaves differently from skin loosening
  gradually with age.

An evaluation determines whether a treatment is appropriate at all. It is not a safety or outcome
guarantee.

## §4 what the practice can look at (S)

**Section heading:** How loose skin is approached here

Where the degree of laxity suits it, energy-based tightening and collagen-stimulating treatment over
a planned series. Where it does not, the evaluation says so. Being told that a treatment will not
reach your expectation is a legitimate and useful outcome of an appointment.

## §8 questions people actually ask (M)

**Section heading:** Common questions

**I lost a lot of weight. Can this be tightened?**
Partially, depending on how much skin there is. Significant loose skin after major weight loss is
generally a surgical question, and you will be told if that is what you are looking at.

**How many sessions before I see anything?**
It is a series, and collagen change is slow. The timeline is set at the evaluation so you are not
judging the result at week two.

**Will it keep improving after the last session?**
Collagen-driven change continues for some months. That is also why the result is assessed at a set
point rather than immediately.

**What does it cost?**
Quote-only pricing. During your consultation, we will build a customized treatment plan with packaged
pricing.
