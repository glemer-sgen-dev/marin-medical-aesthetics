## §1 hero (S)

**H1:** EXION in San Anselmo

A platform combining radiofrequency with targeted ultrasound, used across face and body depending on

---

## §2 the problem it is aimed at (M)

**Section heading:** What people come in with

Because EXION is a platform rather than a single treatment, people arrive for very different reasons:
skin quality and laxity on the face, contour and firmness on the body, or a combination.

That flexibility is also why choosing it from a website is the wrong way round. The applicator is a
decision, and it should follow an assessment rather than precede one.

---

## §3 how this practice handles it (M)

**Section heading:** How treatment here is decided

- **The evaluation covers function as well as appearance**, and determines which application, if any,
  is relevant to what you have.
- **It ends in a written plan you keep**, naming the area, the series and the review point.
- **It decides whether a treatment is appropriate at all.**

An evaluation determines whether a treatment is appropriate at all. It is not a safety or outcome
guarantee.

---

## §4 what the treatment involves (S)

**Section heading:** What a session is like

A handpiece is moved across the treatment area, delivering warmth. Sensation is generally described
as a warm massage. There is typically no numbing and no recovery period, though the area may be pink
afterwards.

It is a series, and the number depends on the area and the goal.

---

## §5 who this is generally for, and what is published about results (S)

**Section heading:** Who it suits, and what is claimed

This practice's patients are typically adults roughly 35-65 across Marin County who want visible
improvement in skin, body contour or pelvic-floor function, and who want a physician evaluating
whether a treatment is appropriate for them at all.

No percentage figures, no outcome figures and no before and after photographs are published, because
none is this practice's own and verified.

---

## §6 what this does not do (S)

**Section heading:** What this is not

- **Not a single treatment with a single result.** What it does depends entirely on the applicator and
  the area.
- **Not a facelift or a body-contouring surgery**, and not an alternative to either.
- **Not a weight-loss treatment.**
- No outcome guarantee, no "typical results" framing.
