## §2 the problem it is aimed at (M)

**Section heading:** What actually changes after pregnancy

Several things at once, which is why single treatments so often disappoint. Abdominal tone that has
not returned. Skin that no longer sits as it did. Fat that has redistributed. And, very commonly and
much less often mentioned, a pelvic floor that no longer holds.

That last one is why this page exists in its current form. Most plans marketed under this heading
address only what is visible, and leave the part that affects a person's day untouched.

---

## §3 how this practice handles it (M)

**Section heading:** How the plan is built

- **The evaluation covers function as well as appearance.** Continence and pelvic-floor strength are
  assessed in the same appointment as tone, skin and contour, not treated as a separate specialty.
- **It ends in a written plan you keep**, sequenced across the areas being addressed.
- **It decides whether a treatment is appropriate at all**, including how long after delivery it is
  reasonable to start and whether anything should be referred first.

An evaluation determines whether a treatment is appropriate at all. It is not a safety or outcome
guarantee.

---

## §4 what a plan can include (S)

**Section heading:** What the plan may cover

Depending entirely on what the evaluation finds, it may combine:

- **Muscle and tone** work for the abdomen.
- **Fat and contour** treatment where a localised area has not responded.
- **Skin quality and firmness** where laxity is present.
- **Pelvic-floor strengthening**, received fully clothed and seated, where continence or strength is
  part of the picture.

It is a plan, not a package. There is no fixed bundle, no tier, and no named tiers on this site.

## §5 who this is generally for, and what is published about results (S)

**Section heading:** Who it suits, and what is claimed

This practice's patients are typically adults roughly 35-65 across Marin County who want visible
improvement in skin, body contour or pelvic-floor function, and who want a physician evaluating

No outcome figures, no percentages and no before and after photographs are published, because none is

## §6 what this is not (S)

**Section heading:** What this page does not offer or claim

- **Not surgery, and not an alternative to it.** Abdominoplasty, liposuction and breast surgery are

- **No "no downtime" or "no side effects" claim.** Both are unqualified absolutes, both appear on the
  existing page, and neither survives here.

## §8 questions people actually ask (M)

**Section heading:** Common questions

**How long after having a baby can I start?**
That is an evaluation question and depends on delivery, recovery and whether you are feeding. No
timeline is published here because it is not a general answer.

**Is this the same as surgery?**
No. It is explicitly non-surgical, which is why the qualifier is in the name. If what you want needs
surgery, you will be told.

**Does it include the pelvic floor?**
It can, and that is much of the point. It is assessed in the same appointment rather than treated as
somebody else's department.

**Is it a set package?**
No. There is no fixed bundle. The plan is built from what the evaluation finds.

**What does it cost?**
Quote-only pricing. During your consultation, we will build a customized treatment plan with packaged
pricing.
