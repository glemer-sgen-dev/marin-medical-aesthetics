Injectable treatment for lines produced by muscle movement, planned and performed by a physician.

---

## §2 the problem it is aimed at (M)

**Section heading:** What people come in with

Lines across the forehead, between the brows and around the eyes that deepen on expression. Often a
sense of looking tired or cross when neither is true.

Many people arrive having had treatment elsewhere that was either too much, too little, or placed
without much thought about how their face actually moves.

---

## §3 how this practice handles it (M)

**Section heading:** How treatment here is decided

- **The evaluation covers function as well as appearance**, and it happens before any product is
  discussed as suitable for you.
- **It ends in a written plan you keep**, including dose, placement and interval.
- **It decides whether a treatment is appropriate at all.** Sometimes the honest recommendation is
  less than you came in expecting, or none.

An evaluation determines whether a treatment is appropriate at all. It is not a safety or outcome
guarantee.

---

## §4 what the treatment involves (S)

**Section heading:** What an appointment is like

A series of small injections into specific muscles, taking a few minutes. Effect develops over
several days rather than immediately, and it wears off gradually, which is why interval matters as
much as dose.

Placement is decided from how your face moves, not from a diagram.

## §5 who this is generally for, and what is published about results (S)

**Section heading:** Who it suits, and what is claimed

This practice's patients are typically adults roughly 35-65 across Marin County who want visible
improvement in skin, body contour or pelvic-floor function, and who want a physician evaluating
whether a treatment is appropriate for them at all.

No before and after photographs are published, because none is on file with a documented patient
release. No outcome figures are published either.

---

## §6 what this does not do (S)

**Section heading:** What this is not

- **Not a treatment for static lines.** Lines etched in at rest soften at best; they need something
  additional.
- **Not a filler.** It relaxes muscle rather than restoring volume, and the two are frequently
  confused.
- **Not permanent**, and not a substitute for skin quality work.
- **Not made safe by a credential.** Harm has occurred with both licensed and unlicensed injectors,
  so no claim of that kind is made here.

## §8 questions people actually ask (M)

**Section heading:** Common questions

**Will I look frozen?**
That result comes from dose and placement, which are clinical decisions. The recommendation here is
frequently more conservative than people expect.

**How long does it last?**
Months rather than weeks, and it varies by person and by area. Your expected interval is part of the
written plan.

**Does it hurt?**
Brief and minor for most people. It is not described as painless, because that would be an
unqualified absolute rather than a description.

**What if I do not like it?**
Effect is temporary and wears off. That is discussed honestly before you agree to anything, including
what can and cannot be adjusted in the meantime.

**What does it cost?**
Quote-only pricing. During your consultation, we will build a customized treatment plan with packaged
pricing.
