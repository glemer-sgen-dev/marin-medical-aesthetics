## §3 document body (XL)

**⚠OWNER PLACEHOLDER, FOR COUNSEL.** Clause headings above, counsel's text underneath. Three
build-side facts are decided upstream rather than by counsel, and the finished policy MUST reflect
all three.

**(a) The collection surface is the consultation and contact request form.**
That is where personal information enters this site. The policy must describe those fields by name:
name, email, phone (optional), the free-text description of what the visitor would like help with,
and preferred contact method. There is no account system, no checkout, and no other collection point
on the site.

**(b) The existing lead-capture forms are vendor-hosted third-party embeds.**
Leads currently land in that third party's system, not the practice's. A processor disclosure is
therefore required, and **the migration decision has to be made before this policy is finalised**.
Whichever processor ends up handling the form is the one the policy has to name. Writing the policy
before that decision would document a data flow that is about to change.

**(c) The loyalty-app download page routes to a third-party app with its own data handling.**
The policy must either cover that app explicitly or explicitly exclude it. Silence is not an option,
because the visitor arrives at it from this site.

**⚠No compliance badge, certification, seal or framework claim appears anywhere on this page.** None
is verified, so none ships. That includes any HIPAA, SOC, ISO, GDPR or privacy-shield style mark or
assertion of adherence.
