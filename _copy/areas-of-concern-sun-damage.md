## §1 hero (S)

**H1:** Sun damage in San Anselmo

The cumulative record of years outdoors: uneven tone, brown patches, roughness, and a surface that no
longer reflects light evenly.

---

## §2 the part that actually wears you down (M)

**Section heading:** Why it seems to arrive all at once

Sun damage accumulates invisibly for decades and then becomes visible over a short period, which is
why it feels sudden. Much of it was acquired long before anyone thought about it, and in Marin the
exposure is year-round rather than seasonal.

It is also the concern most often self-treated with brightening products that address the top layer
of a problem sitting several layers down.

---

## §3 what a physician evaluation changes (M)

**Section heading:** Why this starts with an evaluation

- **Not every brown mark is sun damage.** Some are melasma, which worsens under treatments that suit
  sun damage. Some need looking at for other reasons entirely.
- **Anything suspicious gets named.** A lesion that should be assessed for skin cancer is referred,
  not treated cosmetically. This practice does not offer skin-cancer treatment and does not pretend
  otherwise.
- **Depth decides the approach.** Surface pigment and deeper textural change are different problems
  and are sequenced accordingly.

An evaluation determines whether a treatment is appropriate at all. It is not a safety or outcome
guarantee.

---

## §4 what the practice can look at (S)

**Section heading:** How sun damage is approached here

Depending on what the evaluation finds, the plan may involve broadband light aimed at pigment and
redness, resurfacing where texture as well as tone has changed, and a daily protection plan without
which any of it undoes itself.

## §8 questions people actually ask (M)

**Section heading:** Common questions

**Can the damage be reversed?**
Appearance improves. The history does not reverse, and a practice claiming otherwise is selling
something.

**Should I get a mark checked first?**
If anything has changed in size, shape or colour, yes, and by someone who treats skin cancer. That is
not offered here, and the evaluation will tell you plainly if that is what it sees.

**Will it come back?**
Pigment returns with further exposure. Daily protection is the part of the plan that decides whether
the result holds.

**What does it cost?**
Quote-only pricing. During your consultation, we will build a customized treatment plan with packaged
pricing.
