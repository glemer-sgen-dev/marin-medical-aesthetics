## §2 the part that actually wears you down (M)

**Section heading:** The area that ignores everything that works elsewhere

Submental fullness is famously stubborn. People lose weight everywhere else and watch this stay
exactly as it was, which is what makes it disproportionately frustrating relative to its size.

It also drives more avoidance of photographs and video calls than almost anything else on this list.

---

## §3 what a physician evaluation changes (M)

**Section heading:** Why this starts with an evaluation

- **Fat, skin and muscle are separated.** Fullness under the chin can be fat, loose skin, a muscular
  band, or the position of the jaw itself. They need different responses and one of them needs none of
  the available treatments.
- **Jaw and neck posture are considered.** Some of what reads as a double chin is structural and will
  not change with any treatment offered here.
- **The realistic degree of change is set.** Against your anatomy, not a stock photograph.

An evaluation determines whether a treatment is appropriate at all. It is not a safety or outcome
guarantee.

## §4 what the practice can look at (S)

**Section heading:** How this is approached here

Where fat is the main component and treatment is appropriate, non-surgical fat-reduction and
skin-tightening options are discussed. Where loose skin is the main component, the plan is different
and the expected result is different.

**Not offered here:** liposuction, or any surgical approach. Where that is what your result requires,
the evaluation says so.

## §8 questions people actually ask (M)

**Section heading:** Common questions

**Will losing weight fix it?**
Sometimes, and often not. Submental fat is frequently the last to respond, and for some people it does
not respond at all. That is not a failure of effort.

**Is it fat or loose skin?**
That is the question the evaluation answers, and it changes the plan completely.

**Is this the same as the branded fat-freezing treatment I have seen advertised?**
That treatment is not offered here. What is offered is discussed at the evaluation on its own terms,
rather than by comparison to something this practice does not provide.

**What does it cost?**
Quote-only pricing. During your consultation, we will build a customized treatment plan with packaged
pricing.
