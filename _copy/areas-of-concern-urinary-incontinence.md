## §1 hero (S)

**H1:** Urinary incontinence in San Anselmo

Leaking with a cough, a laugh, a sneeze, a lift, or on the way to the bathroom. It is common, it is
treatable, and it is not something you have to manage quietly for the rest of your life.

---

## §2 the part that actually wears you down (M)

**Section heading:** What this actually costs people

It is rarely the leaking itself. It is the planning around it: knowing where the bathrooms are,
choosing clothes for it, carrying spares, skipping the exercise class, thinking twice about the
trampoline with the grandchildren, drinking less than you should before going out.

Most people wait years to raise it, often because nobody has ever asked. It gets treated as an
inevitable consequence of childbirth or age, and it is neither.

---

## §3 what a physician evaluation changes (M)

**Section heading:** Why this starts with an evaluation

- **The type matters.** Leaking on exertion and leaking with sudden urgency are different problems
  with different answers. Treating one as the other does not work.
- **Some causes need referral, not a device.** Certain presentations need urological or gynaecological
  assessment first. An evaluation is where that gets identified, and this practice will say so.
- **It is a medical assessment, not a booking form.** That is the whole reason it happens here in the
  same appointment as everything else.

An evaluation determines whether a treatment is appropriate at all. It is not a safety or outcome
guarantee.

## §8 questions people actually ask (M)

**Section heading:** Common questions

**Do I have to undress?**
No. The treatment discussed here is received fully clothed and seated.

**Isn't this just what happens after having children?**
It is common after childbirth. That does not make it untreatable, and treating it as inevitable is
the reason most people wait years before asking.

**Won't Kegels fix it?**
Sometimes, and they are worth doing. When they have not worked, it is usually because the exercise is
not reaching the muscle or the problem is a different type of incontinence. That distinction is part
of the evaluation.

**Is this embarrassing to bring up?**
It is a medical concern and it is treated as one here. It sits on this site at the same weight as
skin and body for exactly that reason.

**What does it cost?**
Quote-only pricing. During your consultation, we will build a customized treatment plan with packaged
pricing.
