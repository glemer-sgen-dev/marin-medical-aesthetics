Body treatment combining radiofrequency with targeted pressure, aimed at cellulite and skin quality.

## §2 the problem it is aimed at (M)

**Section heading:** What people come in with

Dimpling through the thighs and buttocks, present regardless of body size, alongside skin that has
lost some firmness over the same area.

Most people arrive having tried creams, brushes and wraps, which address a mechanism that is not the
one producing cellulite.

---

## §3 how this practice handles it (M)

**Section heading:** How treatment here is decided

- **The evaluation covers function as well as appearance**, and separates the tethering structure
  causing dimpling from laxity in the skin above it.
- **It ends in a written plan you keep**, with the series length and review point stated.
- **It decides whether a treatment is appropriate at all.**

An evaluation determines whether a treatment is appropriate at all. It is not a safety or outcome
guarantee.

---

## §4 what the treatment involves (S)

**Section heading:** What a session is like

A handpiece is moved over the treatment area, delivering warmth and pressure together. It is
generally described as a warm deep massage. There is no numbing and no recovery period.

It is a series, spaced over several weeks.

---

## §5 who this is generally for, and what is published about results (S)

**Section heading:** Who it suits, and what is claimed

This practice's patients are typically adults roughly 35-65 across Marin County who want visible
improvement in skin, body contour or pelvic-floor function, and who specifically want a physician
rather than a technician assessing whether a treatment suits them at all.

No percentage figures, no outcome figures and no before and after photographs are published, because
none is this practice's own and verified.

## §6 what this does not do (S)

**Section heading:** What this is not

- **Not an elimination of cellulite.** Appearance improves; the underlying structure remains.
- **Not a weight-loss or fat-reduction treatment.**
- **Not a treatment for significant loose skin.**
- No outcome guarantee, no "typical results" framing.

## §8 questions people actually ask (M)

**Section heading:** Common questions

**Will the cellulite go?**
No. Appearance improves. Any practice offering elimination is describing something other than
cellulite.

**Does it help if I am already lean?**
Cellulite occurs at every body size, so being lean neither prevents it nor rules out treatment.

**Is there downtime?**
None is expected. The area may be warm and slightly pink afterwards.

**What does it cost?**
Quote-only pricing. During your consultation, we will build a customized treatment plan with packaged
pricing.
