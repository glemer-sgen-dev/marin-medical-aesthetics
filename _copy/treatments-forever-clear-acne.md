## §2 the problem it is aimed at (M)

**Section heading:** What people come in with

Active breakouts that have not settled with topical treatment, often in adults who have been cycling
through products for years and are wary of oral medication.

The frustration is usually not the acne alone but the pattern: improvement, relapse, and a growing
suspicion that nothing will hold.

---

## §3 how this practice handles it (M)

**Section heading:** How treatment here is decided

- **The evaluation covers function as well as appearance**, including medications and hormonal factors
  that a device-led consultation does not ask about.
- **It ends in a written plan you keep**, including what to stop using.
- **It decides whether a treatment is appropriate at all**, and severe acne may be better served by a
  referral than by light.

An evaluation determines whether a treatment is appropriate at all. It is not a safety or outcome
guarantee.

---

## §4 what the treatment involves (S)

**Section heading:** What a session is like

Different wavelengths are applied in sequence in one appointment, felt as warmth and a brief snap.
Skin is usually flushed afterwards and settles within a day for most people.

It is delivered as a series. Acne treatment is a course, not an event, and the plan says how many and
over what period.

---

## §5 who this is generally for, and what is published about results (S)

**Section heading:** Who it suits, and what is claimed

This practice's patients are typically adults roughly 35-65 across Marin County who want visible
improvement in skin, body contour or pelvic-floor function, and who specifically want a physician
rather than a technician assessing whether a treatment suits them at all.

Adult acne is a common reason people arrive here. No outcome figures and no before and after
photographs are published, because none is on file with a documented patient release.

---

## §6 what this does not do (S)

**Section heading:** What this is not

- **Not a cure**, and not a permanent end to breakouts.
- **Not a treatment for acne scarring.** Scarring is a separate problem, usually addressed after the
  acne is controlled.
- **Not a replacement for medical management** where acne is severe or scarring rapidly.
- No "clear skin" promise and no outcome guarantee.

## §8 questions people actually ask (M)

**Section heading:** Common questions

**Will this clear my skin?**
No such promise is made. It is one part of a plan, and the plan usually includes changes to what you
are applying at home.

**Can it treat my scars at the same time?**
Generally not, and attempting it tends to waste the scar treatment. Active acne is settled first.

**Is it an alternative to oral medication?**
That is a clinical judgement made at the evaluation, not a claim made in advance on a web page.

**What does it cost?**
Quote-only pricing. During your consultation, we will build a customized treatment plan with packaged
pricing.
