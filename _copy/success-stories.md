## §2 the review corpus as it actually stands (M)

**Section heading:** What patients have actually said

**4.8 across 96 Google reviews.**

The rating is shown as Google reports it and is not rounded up. The platform is named because the
number means nothing without it, and it links out so you can read the reviews at source rather than
take our word for the summary.

**The reviews are not uniformly positive, and this page does not present them as though they were.**
Of those 96 reviews, 6 sit at 2 stars or below: two at 2 stars and four at 1 star, with none at 3 or
4 stars. Nothing has been suppressed, filtered or gated to produce a better average. If you want the
negative ones, they are in the same place as the positive ones.

⚠OWNER / build note, not page copy: the 4.8 was read on the Google Business Profile itself on
2026-08-19. The count of 96 was read live on the Google-sourced mirror, whose own breakdown of
5 star = 90, 4 star = 0, 3 star = 0, 2 star = 2, 1 star = 4 sums to exactly 96 and computes to 4.7708,
which displays as 4.8 at one decimal place. The COUNT remains mirror-sourced. Re-verify by LIVE PAGE
READ only. The search index still serves a stale "82 reviews" title for the very same URL that
live-reads 96, so a search snippet is not verification.

**NO YELP FIGURE SHIPS.** The previously captured Yelp count could not be re-verified, the star
rating was never verified at all, and two Yelp listings are in circulation for this practice, which
would split the corpus. Publishing Google alone is fully compliant: the requirement is that the
figures shown be genuine and unedited, not that every platform be shown.

## §3 the rules every published testimonial ships under (S)

**Section heading:** The standard these are published under

Published here rather than assumed, so you can hold the practice to it:

- No fake, insider, incentivised or employee-written review.
- Nothing solicited in exchange for a discount, a product, or anything else of value.
- No selective gating. Patients are not screened for likely sentiment before being asked.
- Negative reviews are not suppressed, and the rating shown is the one the platform reports.

This is the standard the FTC's trade regulation rule on consumer reviews and testimonials
(16 C.F.R. Part 465, effective 2024-10-21) sets, and it is the standard this page holds itself to.

## §4 patients' own words (S)

**Section heading:** In patients' own words

Where a patient's words are ordinary description, the practice mirrors them in its own register:
approachable, kind, warm, professional. Where a patient's words are a claim, they stay in quotation
marks with the platform, the reviewer and the rating attached, because they are that person's
judgement and not the practice's own assertion.

> "THE BEST MED SPA EVER"
> Google, Esme S., 5 stars

That quotation is a customer's, and it is presented as one. It is not a heading, and it is not
restated as the practice's own claim about itself.
