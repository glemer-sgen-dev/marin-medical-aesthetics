## §2 the part that actually wears you down (M)

**Section heading:** The concern people arrive not having said out loud

Most people raising this have not raised it anywhere else, including with their own doctor. It gets
filed under things that are private, or inevitable, or not serious enough to take up an appointment
with.

It is none of those. Changes after childbirth, surgery or menopause are common, they have physical
causes, and some of them are treatable.

---

## §3 what a physician evaluation changes (M)

**Section heading:** Why this starts with an evaluation

- **The cause is physical and gets treated as such.** Pelvic-floor strength, tissue changes and
  hormonal changes produce different symptoms and need different responses.
- **Some of this needs a different specialist**, and the evaluation will say so rather than offering
  something adjacent. Pain in particular warrants proper investigation.
- **It is a physician appointment.** That is the difference between this being assessed and it being
  sold.

An evaluation determines whether a treatment is appropriate at all. It is not a safety or outcome
guarantee.

**Section heading:** How this is approached here

Where pelvic-floor strength is the relevant factor and treatment is appropriate, non-invasive

## §8 questions people actually ask (M)

**Section heading:** Common questions

**Do I have to undress?**
No. The treatment discussed here is received fully clothed and seated.

**Is this a normal thing to bring up?**
Yes, and it is one of the more common reasons people book. It is on this site, in plain words, so
that raising it does not require you to find the words yourself.

**Is this just about the pelvic floor?**
Not always, which is why it is evaluated rather than assumed. Some causes sit outside what is offered
here and are referred.

**I have pain. Is this the right place?**
Pain needs proper investigation first. Say so at the appointment and you will be pointed to the right
assessment rather than treated.

**What does it cost?**
Quote-only pricing. During your consultation, we will build a customized treatment plan with packaged
pricing.
