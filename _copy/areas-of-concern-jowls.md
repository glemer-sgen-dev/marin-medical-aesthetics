## §2 the part that actually wears you down (M)

**Section heading:** Why the jawline reads as age

The jawline is one of the first things read in a photograph and one of the last things people think
to treat. It changes gradually through a combination of skin laxity, volume shifting, and the
underlying support giving way, which is why no single product addresses it.

---

## §3 what a physician evaluation changes (M)

**Section heading:** Why this starts with an evaluation

- **The contributing factors get separated.** Skin laxity, volume loss and fat position contribute in
  different proportions in different faces, and the proportions decide the plan.
- **The limits of non-surgical treatment are stated.** Beyond a certain degree of laxity, a
  non-surgical approach will not deliver what you are picturing. You will be told where you sit.
- **Referral is a real answer.** This practice does not offer surgery, so where surgery is what the
  result requires, that is said plainly instead of substituting something else.

An evaluation determines whether a treatment is appropriate at all. It is not a safety or outcome
guarantee.

---

## §4 what the practice can look at (S)

**Section heading:** How jowls are approached here

Where a non-surgical approach is appropriate, the plan may involve energy-based skin tightening,
collagen stimulation, or treatment aimed at supporting the surrounding structure. Expectations are
set against your actual starting point rather than against a photograph of someone else's.

## §8 questions people actually ask (M)

**Section heading:** Common questions

**Can this be fixed without surgery?**
Sometimes, partially, and it depends entirely on how much laxity is present. The honest answer for
some people is no, and hearing that from a physician who does not perform surgery is worth
something.

**How much improvement is realistic?**
That is answered against your face at the evaluation, not on this page. Any number quoted before an
examination is invented.

**Is it a series or a single treatment?**
Usually a series. The plan says how many and over what period before you commit.

**What does it cost?**
Quote-only pricing. During your consultation, we will build a customized treatment plan with packaged
pricing.
