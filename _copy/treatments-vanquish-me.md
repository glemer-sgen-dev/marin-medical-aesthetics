## §1 hero (S)

**H1:** Vanquish ME in San Anselmo

Non-contact radiofrequency treatment for fat reduction across a larger area, with nothing touching
the skin.

---

## §2 the problem it is aimed at (M)

**Section heading:** What people come in with

A broad area rather than a small pocket: the abdomen and flanks together, or the thighs, in people
close to a stable weight who have a region that has not responded.

The non-contact aspect matters to people who dislike the idea of anything gripping or suctioning the
skin.

---

## §3 how this practice handles it (M)

**Section heading:** How treatment here is decided

- **The evaluation covers function as well as appearance**, including whether what you are pointing at
  is fat, loose skin or fluid.
- **It ends in a written plan you keep**, with the series and review point stated.
- **It decides whether a treatment is appropriate at all.**

An evaluation determines whether a treatment is appropriate at all. It is not a safety or outcome
guarantee.

---

## §4 what the treatment involves (S)

**Section heading:** What a session is like

You lie under the applicator, which does not touch you. The area warms steadily over the session. It
is generally described as comfortable, and people usually return to normal activity immediately.

It is a series, spaced over several weeks.

---

## §5 who this is generally for, and what is published about results (S)

**Section heading:** Who it suits, and what is claimed

This practice's patients are typically adults roughly 35-65 across Marin County who want visible
improvement in skin, body contour or pelvic-floor function, and who specifically want a physician
rather than a technician assessing whether a treatment suits them at all.

No percentage or inch figures, no outcome figures and no before and after photographs are published,
because none is this practice's own and verified.

---

## §6 what this does not do (S)

**Section heading:** What this is not

- **Not a weight-loss treatment.** No weight outcome is claimed or implied.
- **Not a treatment for loose skin**, which frequently occupies the same area and needs a different
  approach.
- **Not surgery**, and not an alternative to it.
- **Not suitable for everyone.** Contraindications are screened at the evaluation.

**Section heading:** Common questions

No. The applicator sits above the area without contact.

**Will I lose weight?**
No. It targets a specific region and is not a weight-loss treatment.

**How many sessions?**
A series. The number is set at the evaluation rather than advertised here.

**What does it cost?**
Quote-only pricing. During your consultation, we will build a customized treatment plan with packaged
pricing.
