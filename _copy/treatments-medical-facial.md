A clinical facial planned around what the evaluation found, rather than chosen from a fixed menu of

---

## §2 the problem it is aimed at (M)

**Section heading:** What people come in with

Congestion, dullness, and skin that needs maintaining between more substantial treatments. Also
people who want to start somewhere gentle and find out how their skin behaves before committing to
anything larger.

The usual frustration with facials is that they are sold as fixed products with names, so the same
treatment is applied regardless of what the skin in front of the therapist is doing.

---

## §3 how this practice handles it (M)

**Section heading:** How treatment here is decided

- **The evaluation covers function as well as appearance**, and the facial is assembled from what it
  found.
- **It ends in a written plan you keep**, including what to use at home, which usually matters more
  than the appointment itself.
- **It decides whether a treatment is appropriate at all.**

An evaluation determines whether a treatment is appropriate at all. It is not a safety or outcome
guarantee.

---

## §4 what the treatment involves (S)

**Section heading:** What an appointment is like

Cleansing, exfoliation and extraction as appropriate, with actives and finishing steps chosen for
your skin on the day. Skin may be slightly pink afterwards and generally settles quickly.

Some presentations are better treated more gently than expected. That is a clinical decision made
during the appointment, not a downgrade.

---

## §5 who this is generally for, and what is published about results (S)

**Section heading:** Who it suits, and what is claimed

This practice's patients are typically adults roughly 35-65 across Marin County who want visible
improvement in skin, body contour or pelvic-floor function, and who specifically want a physician
rather than a technician assessing whether a treatment suits them at all.

No outcome figures and no before and after photographs are published, because none is on file with a
documented patient release.

---

## §6 what this does not do (S)

**Section heading:** What this is not

- **Not a substitute for treatment of an actual condition.** Acne, rosacea and pigment disorders need
  a plan, not a facial.
- **Not a resurfacing treatment**, and not equivalent to laser or needling.
- **Not a fixed package.** There is no named tier system here.
- No outcome guarantee.

## §8 questions people actually ask (M)

**Section heading:** Common questions

**Is this the same as a spa facial?**
It is planned clinically and adjusted to what your skin is doing, and it sits inside a plan rather
than standing alone.

**Will it clear my breakouts?**
Not on its own. If acne is the actual concern, the evaluation will say so and the plan will be
different.

**How often?**
Where maintenance is useful, an interval is set in the plan rather than sold as a subscription.

**What does it cost?**
Quote-only pricing. During your consultation, we will build a customized treatment plan with packaged
pricing.
