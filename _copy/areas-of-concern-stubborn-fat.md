## §1 hero (S)

**H1:** Stubborn fat in San Anselmo

Pockets that stay put through diet and training, in the same places, regardless of what the scale
says.

---

## §2 the part that actually wears you down (M)

**Section heading:** Doing everything right and seeing nothing move

Fat distribution is largely inherited. You do not get to choose where your body releases it from,
which is why a person can lose weight steadily and watch one area stay exactly as it was.

The common conclusion is that the effort was insufficient. Usually it was not.

---

## §3 what a physician evaluation changes (M)

**Section heading:** Why this starts with an evaluation

- **The tissue gets identified.** What reads as fat can be loose skin, muscle separation, or fluid.
  Each needs a different response.
- **Suitability is assessed properly.** These treatments are for localised pockets in people close to
  a stable weight. They are not weight-loss treatments, and using them as one does not work.
- **Health context is part of it.** A physician assessment covers what a body-contouring consultation
  usually does not.

An evaluation determines whether a treatment is appropriate at all. It is not a safety or outcome
guarantee.

## §4 what the practice can look at (S)

**Section heading:** How this is approached here

## §8 questions people actually ask (M)

**Section heading:** Common questions

**Will I lose weight?**
No. These treatments target a specific area. They are not a route to weight loss and are not offered
as one.

**Do I need to be at a particular weight first?**
Generally these suit people close to a stable weight who have a localised pocket that has not
responded. The evaluation is where that is assessed honestly.

**Is the result permanent?**
Treated fat cells do not return, but remaining fat can still enlarge with weight gain. The result
depends on what happens afterwards.

**What does it cost?**
Quote-only pricing. During your consultation, we will build a customized treatment plan with packaged
pricing.
