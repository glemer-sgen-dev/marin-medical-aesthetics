## §1 hero (S)

**H1:** Broken capillaries in San Anselmo

Fine red or purple vessels visible close to the surface, most often around the nose, cheeks and chin.

---

## §2 the part that actually wears you down (M)

**Section heading:** Why concealer stops working

Individual vessels are sharp-edged and sit under the surface, so foundation blurs them without hiding
them. They also tend to multiply slowly, so the amount of covering required creeps upward over years.

They frequently sit alongside general flushing, which is why people describe the whole area as
"redness" without distinguishing the two.

---

## §3 what a physician evaluation changes (M)

**Section heading:** Why this starts with an evaluation

- **Vessels and background redness are separated.** They respond to different things, and a plan
  addressing only one leaves the other visibly behind.
- **The underlying condition gets identified.** Visible vessels are commonly part of rosacea, which
  changes the plan and the aftercare.
- **Recurrence is planned for.** Treated vessels close; the tendency that produced them does not
  disappear.

An evaluation determines whether a treatment is appropriate at all. It is not a safety or outcome
guarantee.

---

## §4 what the practice can look at (S)

**Section heading:** How visible vessels are approached here

Where appropriate, light-based treatment aimed specifically at vessels, which targets the blood
within them rather than the surrounding skin. Where general flushing is also present, that is
addressed as its own part of the plan.

## §8 questions people actually ask (M)

**Section heading:** Common questions

**Will they come back?**
The treated vessels do not reopen, but new ones can form, especially with rosacea. Maintenance is
discussed as part of the plan rather than presented later as a surprise.

**Is this the same as my general redness?**
Usually not, and that is the most useful distinction on this page. Discrete vessels and diffuse
flushing are separate targets.

**Does it hurt?**
It is usually described as a brief snap. You are told what to expect beforehand.

**What does it cost?**
Quote-only pricing. During your consultation, we will build a customized treatment plan with packaged
pricing.
