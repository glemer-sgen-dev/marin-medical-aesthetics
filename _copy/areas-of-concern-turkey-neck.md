## §2 the part that actually wears you down (M)

**Section heading:** The part people cover rather than treat

The neck is where people notice the gap between how old they feel and how old they look. It is also
routinely left out of a skincare routine that stops at the jawline, and it shows the omission.

Scarves, collars and camera angles are the usual response, for years, before anyone asks whether
something can be done.

## §3 what a physician evaluation changes (M)

**Section heading:** Why this starts with an evaluation

- **Skin, muscle and fat are assessed separately.** Vertical banding is muscular, crepey texture is
  skin, and fullness under the chin is neither. They need different answers.
- **The ceiling gets named.** Non-surgical treatment has a limit here, and where your expectation
  sits beyond it you will be told, not sold a series that cannot reach it.
- **This practice does not perform surgery**, which removes any incentive to talk you toward or away
  from it.

An evaluation determines whether a treatment is appropriate at all. It is not a safety or outcome
guarantee.

## §8 questions people actually ask (M)

**Section heading:** Common questions

**Are the vertical bands the same as loose skin?**
No. Banding is muscle and loose skin is skin. A plan aimed at one does little for the other, which is
why they are separated at the evaluation.

**Does the neck respond as well as the face?**
Generally less well and more slowly. Anyone promising you facial results on the neck is not
describing the tissue accurately.

**How many sessions?**
It is a series, and the number is set at the evaluation rather than guessed here.

**What does it cost?**
Quote-only pricing. During your consultation, we will build a customized treatment plan with packaged
pricing.
