## §1 hero (S)

**H1:** Rosacea in San Anselmo

Persistent redness, flushing, and visible vessels across the cheeks, nose and chin. It comes and goes
on its own schedule, and it rarely responds to the things that work on other skin.

---

## §2 the part that actually wears you down (M)

**Section heading:** What makes rosacea difficult

It is not only how it looks. It is that it moves. A face that is calm in the morning is flushed by
the afternoon, and the trigger is often something ordinary: a hot room, a glass of wine, exercise, a
change in weather, or nothing identifiable at all.

Most people arrive here having already tried a shelf of products, some of which made it worse.
Rosacea is not a cleansing problem, and treating it as one costs time.

---

## §3 what a physician evaluation changes (M)

**Section heading:** Why this starts with an evaluation

- **What is actually happening gets identified first.** Redness, flushing and visible vessels are not
  one condition, and they do not all respond to the same thing. Naming which pattern you have is the
  first useful step.
- **Your triggers and your history are part of the assessment.** Rosacea interacts with medications,
  other skin conditions and general health, which is why a physician assessment covers more than the
  surface.
- **You get a written plan you keep**, including what to stop doing, not only what to start.

An evaluation determines whether a treatment is appropriate at all. It is not a safety or outcome
guarantee.

## §8 questions people actually ask (M)

**Section heading:** Common questions

**Will this get rid of it permanently?**
No. Rosacea is a chronic condition that is managed. The realistic goal is fewer and less severe
flares and less visible background redness, maintained over time.

**Is it the same as acne?**
No, although the two are confused constantly and some treatments for one make the other worse. That
is a large part of why an evaluation comes first.

**Do I need to stop using my current products?**
Possibly, and that is part of the plan. Some common actives aggravate rosacea. The evaluation covers
what to stop as well as what to start.

**What does it cost?**
Quote-only pricing. During your consultation, we will build a customized treatment plan with packaged
pricing.
