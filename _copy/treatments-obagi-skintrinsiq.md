## §1 hero (S)

**H1:** Obagi SkinTrinsiq in San Anselmo

A device-assisted treatment that cleanses and then infuses actives into the skin, used as preparation
or maintenance alongside other treatments.

---

## §2 the problem it is aimed at (M)

**Section heading:** What people come in with

Congestion and dullness, and the common problem of a home routine that is not penetrating far enough
to change anything.

It is often chosen by people between larger treatments who want the interval to be doing something
rather than nothing.

---

## §3 how this practice handles it (M)

**Section heading:** How treatment here is decided

- **The evaluation covers function as well as appearance**, including which actives suit your skin and
  which are aggravating it.
- **It ends in a written plan you keep**, covering both the appointment and the home routine.
- **It decides whether a treatment is appropriate at all.**

An evaluation determines whether a treatment is appropriate at all. It is not a safety or outcome
guarantee.

---

## §4 what the treatment involves (S)

**Section heading:** What an appointment is like

Two phases: cleansing and extraction, then infusion of actives selected for your skin. It is
comfortable, there is no numbing, and people return to normal activity immediately.

---

## §5 who this is generally for, and what is published about results (S)

**Section heading:** Who it suits, and what is claimed

This practice's patients are typically adults roughly 35-65 across Marin County who want visible
improvement in skin, body contour or pelvic-floor function, and who specifically want a physician
rather than a technician assessing whether a treatment suits them at all.

No outcome figures and no before and after photographs are published, because none is on file with a
documented patient release.

## §6 what this does not do (S)

**Section heading:** What this is not

- **Not a resurfacing treatment** and not equivalent to laser or needling.
- **Not a treatment for an active skin condition** on its own.
- **Not a product endorsement.** Naming the system describes what is used here; it is not a claim
  about the brand's superiority over any other.
- No outcome guarantee.

## §8 questions people actually ask (M)

**Section heading:** Common questions

**Do I have to buy the product range?**
No. What you use at home is part of the plan and is chosen for your skin, not bundled as a condition
of treatment.

**Is it a facial?**
It overlaps with one. The difference is the infusion phase and the fact that the actives are selected
clinically.

**How often?**
Where useful, an interval is set in the plan.

**What does it cost?**
Quote-only pricing. During your consultation, we will build a customized treatment plan with packaged
pricing.
