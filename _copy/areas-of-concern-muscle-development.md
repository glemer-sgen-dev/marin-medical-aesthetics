## §2 the part that actually wears you down (M)

**Section heading:** The plateau

Progress in a specific area often stalls while everything else continues to improve. The training is
consistent, the diet is unchanged, and one region simply refuses to follow.

That plateau is usually a recruitment and volume problem rather than a motivation problem, which is
not how it feels at the time.

---

## §3 what a physician evaluation changes (M)

**Section heading:** Why this starts with an evaluation

- **What is limiting the result gets identified.** Muscle volume, a layer of fat over it, or loose
  skin above it all produce "no definition" and need different responses.
- **Suitability and safety are assessed.** These treatments are not appropriate for everyone, and
  certain implants and medical devices are contraindications. A physician assessment covers that.
- **It is positioned as a supplement.** Treatment supports training; it does not stand in for it.

An evaluation determines whether a treatment is appropriate at all. It is not a safety or outcome
guarantee.

## §8 questions people actually ask (M)

**Section heading:** Common questions

**Is this instead of the gym?**
No, and it is not offered as such. If you are looking for a replacement for training, this is not
one.

**How many sessions?**
It is a series. The number and the interval are set at the evaluation rather than advertised here.

**Are there reasons I could not have it?**
Yes. Certain implanted devices and metal implants are contraindications, among others. That is
exactly the kind of thing a physician evaluation is for.

**What does it cost?**
Quote-only pricing. During your consultation, we will build a customized treatment plan with packaged
pricing.
