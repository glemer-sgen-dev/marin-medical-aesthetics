## §2 why the concern comes before the device (M)

**Section heading:** Concern first, then evaluation, then a plan

You name what is bothering you. A physician evaluates what is actually going on and decides what is
appropriate. The output is a written plan you keep. That order is the whole architecture of this
practice, and it is why the concern list, not the device list, is the thing you meet first.

The evaluation reaches further than the mirror. In the practice's own published words: as part of
any treatment, she does a comprehensive health evaluation to minimize side effects and teaches
clients how their actions can maximize and sustain the benefits they achieve.

An evaluation determines whether a treatment is appropriate at all. It is not a safety or outcome
guarantee.
