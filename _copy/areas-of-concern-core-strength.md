Abdominal tone that exercise alone has not restored, often after pregnancy, surgery or a long period

---

## §2 the part that actually wears you down (M)

**Section heading:** Working the core and not reaching it

Core work assumes the muscle responds to the signal. After pregnancy or abdominal surgery, or where
the abdominal muscles have separated, that assumption often fails: the effort goes in and the muscle
that needs recruiting is not the one doing the work.

The result is months of training with a stomach that does not change, and a reasonable conclusion
that you are doing it wrong.

---

## §3 what a physician evaluation changes (M)

**Section heading:** Why this starts with an evaluation

- **Separation gets checked for.** Abdominal separation changes what exercise is appropriate, and
  some standard core exercises make it worse.
- **Strength, skin and fat get separated.** A stomach that will not flatten can be any of the three,
  or a combination, and they respond differently.
- **The pelvic floor is assessed with it.** Core and pelvic floor work together, and treating one
  while ignoring the other is a common and expensive omission.

An evaluation determines whether a treatment is appropriate at all. It is not a safety or outcome
guarantee.

## §4 what the practice can look at (S)

**Section heading:** How this is approached here

Where appropriate, treatment that stimulates the muscle directly rather than relying on voluntary
recruitment, planned as a series. It supplements training rather than replacing it, and the plan says
so.

## §6 what this page will not claim (S)

**Section heading:** What is not claimed here

- **This does not replace exercise.** It supplements it.
- No weight-loss claim, and no abdominal separation repair claim. Significant separation may be a
  surgical question, and where it is, the evaluation says so.
- No outcome guarantee, no "typical results" framing, no percentage or session-count figures.
- No before and after photographs, because none is published with a documented patient release yet.

## §8 questions people actually ask (M)

**Section heading:** Common questions

**I train hard already. Why would this add anything?**
Because the limiting factor may not be effort. Where a muscle is not being recruited properly,
additional training does not fix the recruitment problem.

**Can this repair abdominal separation?**
No such claim is made. Separation is assessed, it changes what is appropriate, and significant
separation may be a surgical question.

**Do I still need to exercise?**
Yes. This is not a substitute and is not offered as one.

**What does it cost?**
Quote-only pricing. During your consultation, we will build a customized treatment plan with packaged
pricing.
