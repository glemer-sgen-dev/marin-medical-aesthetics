## §2 who this is for, and who it is not for (M)

**Section heading:** Whether this is relevant to you yet

**It is for existing patients.** If you have been seen at the practice and have a plan underway, the
app is where the practical side of that lives.

**It is not a way to become a patient.** If you have not been seen yet, the app is not the starting
point and installing it will not get you an appointment sooner. The starting point is a consultation.

The people this practice sees are typically adults roughly 35-65 across Marin County who want visible
improvement in skin, body contour or pelvic-floor function, and who specifically want a physician
rather than a technician assessing whether a treatment suits them at all. If that describes you and
you have not booked yet, book rather than download.

---

## §3 what the app is for (M)

**Section heading:** What it does

The practice uses RepeatMD. Within it you can keep track of your treatment plan and appointments and
access the rewards and membership offers the practice runs.

It is a convenience layer over care that has already been planned. It does not replace the written
plan you leave your evaluation with, and it does not provide clinical advice.

An evaluation determines whether a treatment is appropriate at all. It is not a safety or outcome
guarantee.

⚠OWNER: the app is a third party with its own data handling. The privacy policy must either cover it
explicitly or explicitly exclude it. That decision has not been made yet and is not asserted here.

## §4 how to get it, and what it costs (S)

**Section heading:** Getting it

Search for the practice within the RepeatMD app, or ask at your next appointment and you will be
walked through it.

On cost of treatment, nothing changes because you have the app: quote-only pricing. During your
consultation, we will build a customized treatment plan with packaged pricing.

⚠OWNER: no promotional value, discount, points figure or reward amount is published here. None has
been verified, and the `$500 OFF` promotion carried on a retiring property has restrictions that live
on a separate page. It is not lifted onto this site without its terms adjacent and legible.
