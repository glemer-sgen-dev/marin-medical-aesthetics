## §1 hero (S)

**H1:** Forever Young BBL in San Anselmo

Broadband light directed at pigment, redness and the visible marks of sun exposure.

---

## §2 the problem it is aimed at (M)

**Section heading:** What people come in with

Brown spots, blotchy tone and background redness across the face, chest or hands, usually
accumulated over decades rather than arriving recently.

The chest and hands matter here: they are the areas people cannot easily cover and the areas most
often left out of a skincare routine entirely.

---

## §3 how this practice handles it (M)

**Section heading:** How treatment here is decided

- **The evaluation covers function as well as appearance**, and sorts which marks are suitable for
  light-based treatment and which need looking at for other reasons.
- **It ends in a written plan you keep.**
- **It decides whether a treatment is appropriate at all.**

An evaluation determines whether a treatment is appropriate at all. It is not a safety or outcome
guarantee.

---

## §4 what the treatment involves (S)

**Section heading:** What a session is like

Pulses of light are applied across the treatment area, felt as a brief snap. Treated brown spots
typically darken over the following days before flaking away, and redness settles gradually.

It is delivered as a series, with maintenance discussed as part of the plan rather than raised later.

---

## §5 who this is generally for, and what is published about results (S)

**Section heading:** Who it suits, and what is claimed

This practice's patients are typically adults roughly 35-65 across Marin County who want visible
improvement in skin, body contour or pelvic-floor function, and who want a physician evaluating
whether a treatment is appropriate for them at all.

No outcome figures and no before and after photographs are published, because none is on file with a
documented patient release.

---

## §6 what this does not do (S)

**Section heading:** What this is not

- **Not a resurfacing treatment.** It addresses colour more than texture.
- **Not suitable for every skin type.** Light-based pigment treatment carries real risk in deeper skin
  tones, which is a physician assessment rather than a setting.
- **Not a screening service.** Suspicious lesions are referred; skin-cancer treatment is not offered
  here.
- **Not permanent.** New sun-driven pigment forms with further exposure.

## §8 questions people actually ask (M)

**Section heading:** Common questions

**Why do the spots get darker first?**
That is the expected response when pigment is targeted, and it typically resolves over days. It is
explained before treatment, not after.

**Can my hands and chest be treated?**
Often, yes, and they are among the more common requests.

**Is it safe for my skin tone?**
That is assessed rather than assumed. No treatment here is described as suitable for every skin type.

**Will it stop new spots forming?**
No. Sun protection is the part of the plan that determines whether the result holds.

**What does it cost?**
Quote-only pricing. During your consultation, we will build a customized treatment plan with packaged
pricing.
