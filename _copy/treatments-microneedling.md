## §1 hero (S)

**H1:** Microneedling in San Anselmo

Controlled micro-injury that prompts the skin to rebuild collagen, aimed at texture and scarring.

---

## §2 the problem it is aimed at (M)

**Section heading:** What people come in with

Acne scarring, enlarged pores, and a surface that no longer reflects light evenly. Often people who
have improved tone with other treatments and found that texture did not follow.

It is also one of the most common at-home treatments, usually with a device that does not reach the
depth required, which is worth knowing before assuming it does not work.

---

## §3 how this practice handles it (M)

**Section heading:** How treatment here is decided

- **The evaluation covers function as well as appearance**, including whether active acne needs
  settling before scarring is treated.
- **It ends in a written plan you keep**, including the number of sessions and their spacing.
- **It decides whether a treatment is appropriate at all.**

An evaluation determines whether a treatment is appropriate at all. It is not a safety or outcome

---

## §4 what the treatment involves (S)

**Section heading:** What a session is like

Numbing cream first, then fine needles create controlled micro-channels at a chosen depth. Skin is
red and warm afterwards, similar to sunburn, usually settling within a day or two.

It is a series, spaced to allow collagen to respond between sessions.

This practice's patients are typically adults roughly 35-65 across Marin County who want visible

## §6 what this does not do (S)

**Section heading:** What this is not

- **Not a treatment for active acne.** Needling through active breakouts can spread inflammation, so
  acne is settled first.
- **Not scar removal.** Scarring is improved in appearance, not erased.
- **Not the same as an at-home roller**, which works at a fraction of the depth.
- No outcome guarantee, no "typical results" framing.

## §8 questions people actually ask (M)

**Section heading:** Common questions

**Is this the same as my at-home roller?**
No. Depth is the variable that matters, and home devices work well above the level at which collagen
remodelling happens.

**Can it be done while I am breaking out?**
Generally not. Active acne is settled first, because needling through it risks spreading
inflammation.

**Will my scars go?**
They improve in appearance. Removal is not a claim made here.

**How many sessions?**
A series, spaced apart. The number is set at the evaluation.

**What does it cost?**
Quote-only pricing. During your consultation, we will build a customized treatment plan with packaged
pricing.
