It is passive and comfortable. There is no recovery period.

## §8 questions people actually ask (M)

**Section heading:** Common questions

**Will this help my insomnia?**
No claim is made about insomnia as a condition. It is offered as a relaxation session, and persistent
sleep problems deserve a proper medical assessment.

**Is it a medical treatment?**
No. It is a wellness session and is described as one.

**Is it safe if I have epilepsy or a seizure history?**
Say so before booking. Light-based sessions are not appropriate for everyone and that is screened.

**What does it cost?**
Quote-only pricing. During your consultation, we will build a customized treatment plan with packaged
pricing.
