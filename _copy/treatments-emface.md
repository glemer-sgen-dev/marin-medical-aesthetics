## §1 hero (S)

**H1:** EmFace in San Anselmo

Needle-free facial treatment combining radiofrequency with muscle stimulation, applied through pads
placed on the face.

---

## §2 the problem it is aimed at (M)

**Section heading:** What people come in with

Softening definition and early laxity in people who do not want injections, or who have had them and
want to add support rather than more product.

It is also frequently the request of people who want to change something without anyone being able to
identify what changed.

---

## §3 how this practice handles it (M)

**Section heading:** How treatment here is decided

- **The evaluation covers function as well as appearance**, including whether muscle or skin is the
  larger contributor in your face.
- **It ends in a written plan you keep.**
- **It decides whether a treatment is appropriate at all**, including screening for contraindications.

An evaluation determines whether a treatment is appropriate at all. It is not a safety or outcome
guarantee.

---

## §4 what the treatment involves (S)

**Section heading:** What a session is like

Pads are placed on the forehead and cheeks. You feel warmth and rhythmic muscle contraction. There
are no needles and no numbing, and people generally return to normal activity straight afterwards.

It is a short series, typically spaced over a few weeks, with change developing afterwards rather
than during.

---

## §5 who this is generally for, and what is published about results (S)

**Section heading:** Who it suits, and what is claimed

This practice's patients are typically adults roughly 35-65 across Marin County who want visible
improvement in skin, body contour or pelvic-floor function, and who want a physician evaluating
whether a treatment is appropriate for them at all.

No outcome figures and no before and after photographs are published, because none is on file with a
documented patient release. This device category is marketed heavily on percentage figures; none is
this practice's own, so none appears here.

---

## §6 what this does not do (S)

**Section heading:** What this is not

- **Not a facelift**, and not an alternative to one. Surgery is not offered here.
- **Not a replacement for injectables**, though it is sometimes chosen instead of them. Which suits
  you is the evaluation's decision, not a marketing position.
- **Not suitable for everyone.** Certain implanted electronic and metal devices are contraindications.
- No outcome guarantee and no percentage claims.

## §8 questions people actually ask (M)

**Section heading:** Common questions

**Are there needles?**
No. Pads are placed on the skin.

**Is it instead of injectables?**
Sometimes, sometimes alongside, and sometimes neither is appropriate. That is decided at the
evaluation.

**Are there reasons I could not have it?**
Yes, including certain implanted devices. Screening is part of the evaluation.

**When would I notice anything?**
Change develops over the weeks after the series rather than during it.

**What does it cost?**
Quote-only pricing. During your consultation, we will build a customized treatment plan with packaged
pricing.
