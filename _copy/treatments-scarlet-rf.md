## §2 the problem it is aimed at (M)

**Section heading:** What people come in with

Early laxity along the jaw and neck, softening definition, and texture that surface treatments have
improved but not resolved. Often people who feel the change is structural rather than superficial and
who are not ready to consider surgery.

**Section heading:** How treatment here is decided

- **The evaluation covers function as well as appearance**, including how much laxity is actually

- **It ends in a written plan you keep**, including the number of sessions and the review point.

An evaluation determines whether a treatment is appropriate at all. It is not a safety or outcome
guarantee.

---

## §4 what the treatment involves (S)

**Section heading:** What a session is like

Numbing cream first. Fine needles enter the skin and deliver radiofrequency energy at a set depth,
felt as heat and pressure. Skin is red and warm afterwards, usually settling over a day or two, with
small marks possible.

Collagen change is slow, so results are assessed months rather than days after the series.

This practice's patients are typically adults roughly 35-65 across Marin County who want visible

## §6 what this does not do (S)

**Section heading:** What this is not

- **Not a facelift**, and not presented as an alternative to one. Surgery is not offered here.
- **Not immediate.** The mechanism is collagen remodelling, which takes months.
- **Not a no-downtime treatment.** Redness and small marks are expected and described in advance.
- No outcome guarantee, no "typical results" framing.

## §8 questions people actually ask (M)

**Section heading:** Common questions

**How is this different from ordinary microneedling?**
It adds radiofrequency energy at depth, so it targets laxity as well as texture. Which is appropriate
for you depends on what the evaluation finds.

**When will I see something?**
Not immediately. Collagen change is gradual, and the review point is set in the plan so you are not
judging it too early.

**How many sessions?**
A series. The number is set at the evaluation.

**Does it hurt?**
Numbing cream is used. It is described as heat and pressure rather than as painless.

**What does it cost?**
Quote-only pricing. During your consultation, we will build a customized treatment plan with packaged
pricing.
