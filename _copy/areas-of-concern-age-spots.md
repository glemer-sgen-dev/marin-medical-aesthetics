## §1 hero (S)

**H1:** Age spots in San Anselmo

Discrete brown marks, usually flat, most often on the face, chest and backs of the hands. They sit
where the sun reached most often over the longest time.

---

## §2 the part that actually wears you down (M)

**Section heading:** Why they bother people more than the size suggests

They are small, and they are the thing people notice in photographs. They also appear on the hands
and chest, which makeup does not practically cover, so they are the part of ageing that is hardest to
manage privately.

---

## §3 what a physician evaluation changes (M)

**Section heading:** Why this starts with an evaluation

- **A brown mark is not automatically an age spot.** Some are melasma, which responds badly to
  treatments that suit age spots. Some warrant assessment for other reasons.
- **Anything suspicious is referred rather than treated.** Skin-cancer treatment is not offered here,
  and a mark that has changed is not a cosmetic problem until someone qualified says it is.
- **The hands and chest are not the face.** They heal differently and are treated with different
  settings and expectations.

An evaluation determines whether a treatment is appropriate at all. It is not a safety or outcome
guarantee.

---

## §4 what the practice can look at (S)

**Section heading:** How age spots are approached here

Where appropriate, light-based treatment directed at the pigment itself, planned around the area
being treated. Marks typically darken before they clear, which is expected and is explained before
you agree to anything rather than after.

## §8 questions people actually ask (M)

**Section heading:** Common questions

**Why do they get darker before they fade?**
That is the expected response when pigment is targeted. It is explained in advance, along with how
long it usually lasts, so it is not a surprise on day three.

**Can the hands be treated?**
Often, yes, and they are one of the more common requests. They are planned differently from the face.

**Will they come back?**
New ones form with further exposure. Sun protection is part of the plan, not an afterthought.

**Should I get a spot checked first?**
If it has changed in size, shape or colour, yes, and by someone who treats skin cancer. That is not
offered here.

**What does it cost?**
Quote-only pricing. During your consultation, we will build a customized treatment plan with packaged
pricing.
