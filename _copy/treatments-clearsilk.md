A gentle laser treatment aimed at background redness, tone and overall skin quality, with little

---

## §2 the problem it is aimed at (M)

**Section heading:** What people come in with

Diffuse redness that foundation flattens rather than fixes. Dullness. Skin that looks tired in
photographs without any single feature being wrong.

It is also frequently the request of people who want an improvement but cannot take visible recovery
time, which historically forced a choice between doing nothing and doing something disruptive.

---

## §3 how this practice handles it (M)

**Section heading:** How treatment here is decided

- **The evaluation covers function as well as appearance**, and decides whether this is the right tool
  for what you have.
- **It ends in a written plan you keep.**
- **It decides whether a treatment is appropriate at all**, including whether a gentler or a stronger
  approach is the honest recommendation.

An evaluation determines whether a treatment is appropriate at all. It is not a safety or outcome
guarantee.

---

## §4 what the treatment involves (S)

**Section heading:** What a session is like

The laser is passed over the skin, warming it gradually. Most people describe it as a warm, tolerable
sensation rather than a painful one. Skin is usually slightly flushed afterwards and settles quickly.

It is delivered as a series, because the change is cumulative rather than immediate.

---

## §5 who this is generally for, and what is published about results (S)

**Section heading:** Who it suits, and what is claimed

This practice's patients are typically adults roughly 35-65 across Marin County who want visible
improvement in skin, body contour or pelvic-floor function, and who want a physician evaluating
whether a treatment is appropriate for them at all.

No outcome figures and no before and after photographs are published, because none is on file with a
documented patient release.

---

## §6 what this does not do (S)

**Section heading:** What this is not

- **Not a resurfacing treatment.** It works gently and cumulatively; it does not do what an ablative
  treatment does.
- **Not a single-session fix**, and not a treatment for deep textural scarring.
- **No "zero downtime" claim.** Flushing afterwards is normal and is described honestly rather than
  denied.

## §8 questions people actually ask (M)

**Section heading:** Common questions

**Can I go back to work afterwards?**
Usually, yes. Skin is often flushed for a period. That is described in the plan rather than promised
away.

**How many sessions?**
It is a series, and the number is set at the evaluation.

**Is it suitable for my skin tone?**
That is a physician assessment. No treatment on this site is described as suitable for every skin
type, because none is.

**What does it cost?**
Quote-only pricing. During your consultation, we will build a customized treatment plan with packaged
pricing.
