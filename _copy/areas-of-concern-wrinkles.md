## §2 the part that actually wears you down (M)

**Section heading:** Why the same treatment stops working

A line that was once only there when you smiled becomes a line that is always there. At that point
relaxing the muscle softens it but no longer removes it, because the skin itself has changed.

This is where people conclude the treatment has stopped working, when what has actually happened is
that the problem changed category.

---

## §3 what a physician evaluation changes (M)

**Section heading:** Why this starts with an evaluation

- **Static lines need a different plan.** Muscle treatment alone plateaus once a line is etched in.
- **Volume and support get assessed too.** Some lines are the visible edge of volume loss elsewhere
  and do not respond to being treated directly.
- **Sequence and expectation get set honestly.** Etched lines usually soften rather than disappear,
  and that is said before you spend anything.

An evaluation determines whether a treatment is appropriate at all. It is not a safety or outcome
guarantee.

## §4 what the practice can look at (S)

**Section heading:** How wrinkles are approached here

Depending on what the evaluation finds, a plan may combine treatment aimed at movement with work
directed at the skin itself, such as resurfacing or collagen stimulation, in an order that gives each
part a chance to do its job.

## §8 questions people actually ask (M)

**Section heading:** Common questions

**Why does my treatment not work as well as it used to?**
Usually because the line has become static. The muscle is still being relaxed; the etched crease in
the skin needs something else in addition.

**Can they be removed completely?**
Established wrinkles soften. Removal is not a claim this practice makes, and where a result would
need surgery to achieve, you will be told that rather than sold an approximation.

**Is more product the answer?**
Rarely, and it is the most common route to an over-treated appearance.

**What does it cost?**
Quote-only pricing. During your consultation, we will build a customized treatment plan with packaged
pricing.
