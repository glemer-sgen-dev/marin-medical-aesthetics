Threadlike red, blue or purple vessels branching close to the surface, on the face or the legs.

---

## §2 the part that actually wears you down (M)

**Section heading:** Why they change what you wear

Leg veins in particular tend to decide clothing. People stop wearing shorts, dresses and swimwear
long before the veins are extensive, and rarely mention why.

They also tend to be dismissed as purely cosmetic, which means the question of whether something else
is going on often never gets asked.

---

## §3 what a physician evaluation changes (M)

**Section heading:** Why this starts with an evaluation

- **Face and legs are different problems.** Facial vessels and leg veins have different causes and
  different treatments; being told they are the same thing is a sign nobody looked properly.
- **Underlying venous disease has to be considered.** Leg veins can indicate circulation problems
  that need vascular assessment rather than cosmetic treatment. A physician evaluation is where that
  question gets raised, and referral is a real outcome.
- **Aching or swelling changes everything.** Symptoms move this out of the cosmetic category.

An evaluation determines whether a treatment is appropriate at all. It is not a safety or outcome
guarantee.

## §4 what the practice can look at (S)

**Section heading:** How spider veins are approached here

For facial vessels, light-based treatment aimed at the vessel itself. For leg veins, the evaluation
comes first and may conclude that vascular assessment is the appropriate next step rather than
treatment here. That is a genuine possible outcome and it is stated up front.

## §8 questions people actually ask (M)

**Section heading:** Common questions

**Are leg veins and facial veins treated the same way?**
No. They differ in cause, depth and treatment, and leg veins carry a question about circulation that
facial vessels do not.

**My legs ache as well. Does that matter?**
Yes, and it is worth saying at the appointment. Aching, swelling or heaviness suggests this is not
purely cosmetic and may need vascular assessment first.

**Will they come back?**
New vessels can form. This is managed rather than solved permanently.

**What does it cost?**
Quote-only pricing. During your consultation, we will build a customized treatment plan with packaged
pricing.
