## §1 hero (S)

**H1:** Emsculpt NEO in San Anselmo

Non-invasive body treatment that combines radiofrequency heating with intense muscle contraction in
the same session.

---

## §2 the problem it is aimed at (M)

**Section heading:** What people come in with

A specific area that has stopped responding: abdomen, buttocks, arms or thighs. Usually people who
train consistently and have hit a plateau in one region while everything else has progressed.

Frequently also people recovering the abdominal strength they had before pregnancy or surgery.

---

## §3 how this practice handles it (M)

**Section heading:** How treatment here is decided

- **The evaluation covers function as well as appearance**, including whether muscle, fat or loose
  skin is the actual limiting factor.
- **It ends in a written plan you keep**, with the number of sessions and the review point set out.
- **It decides whether a treatment is appropriate at all**, including screening for contraindications.

An evaluation determines whether a treatment is appropriate at all. It is not a safety or outcome
guarantee.

---

## §4 what the treatment involves (S)

**Section heading:** What a session is like

Applicators are strapped to the treatment area. You feel heat alongside strong involuntary muscle
contractions. It is unusual rather than painful, and people generally describe muscle soreness
afterwards similar to a hard session at the gym.

It is a short series, spaced over a few weeks.

---

## §5 who this is generally for, and what is published about results (S)

**Section heading:** Who it suits, and what is claimed

This practice's patients are typically adults roughly 35-65 across Marin County who want visible
improvement in skin, body contour or pelvic-floor function, and who want a physician evaluating
whether a treatment is appropriate for them at all.

**No percentage figures for muscle gain or fat reduction are published here.** This device is
marketed on such figures; they are the manufacturer's, not this practice's, and no outcome figure has
been verified for this practice. No before and after photographs either, for the same reason.

---

## §6 what this does not do (S)

**Section heading:** What this is not

- **Not a weight-loss treatment**, and no weight outcome is claimed.
- **Not a replacement for exercise.** It supplements training.
- **Not a treatment for loose skin**, which is a different problem in the same area.
- **Not suitable for everyone.** Certain implanted metal and electronic devices are contraindications.

## §8 questions people actually ask (M)

**Section heading:** Common questions

**Will I lose weight?**
No. This targets a specific area and is not offered as a route to weight loss.

**Does it replace the gym?**
No, and it is not sold as such.

**What does it feel like?**
Heat and strong contractions, then soreness afterwards. It is not described as painless.

**Are there reasons I could not have it?**
Yes, including certain implants and metal devices. That screening is part of the evaluation.

**What does it cost?**
Quote-only pricing. During your consultation, we will build a customized treatment plan with packaged
pricing.
