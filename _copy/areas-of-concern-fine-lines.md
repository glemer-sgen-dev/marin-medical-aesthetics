The earliest lines, usually where the face moves most: around the eyes, across the forehead, between

---

## §2 the part that actually wears you down (M)

**Section heading:** The stage where it is easy to overdo it

Fine lines are the point at which most people first consider treatment, and the point at which the
wrong amount of it is most obvious. Too much, too early, on a face that mostly needs skin quality
rather than muscle relaxation, is the result everyone is trying to avoid.

---

## §3 what a physician evaluation changes (M)

**Section heading:** Why this starts with an evaluation

- **Movement lines and static lines are distinguished.** Lines that appear only on expression respond
  differently from lines etched into the skin at rest.
- **Skin quality is assessed separately from muscle.** Many early lines are a collagen and texture
  problem wearing a muscle problem's clothing.
- **Restraint is a plan, not a lack of one.** The recommendation may be less than you expected, and
  that is a clinical position rather than a soft sell.

An evaluation determines whether a treatment is appropriate at all. It is not a safety or outcome
guarantee.

---

## §4 what the practice can look at (S)

**Section heading:** How fine lines are approached here

Depending on which type is present, the plan may involve treatment aimed at expression lines,
collagen-stimulating or resurfacing work where the skin itself is the issue, or both in sequence.
Which applies to you is the output of the evaluation.

## §8 questions people actually ask (M)

**Section heading:** Common questions

**Am I too young for this?**
It is not an age question. It is a question about which lines you have and whether anything is
appropriate, which is what the evaluation answers.

**Will my face look frozen?**
That outcome comes from too much product in the wrong places. Dose and placement are clinical
decisions, and the recommendation here is frequently more conservative than people expect.

**Is skincare enough?**
Sometimes, particularly early. If that is the honest answer, it is the one you will be given.

**What does it cost?**
Quote-only pricing. During your consultation, we will build a customized treatment plan with packaged
pricing.
