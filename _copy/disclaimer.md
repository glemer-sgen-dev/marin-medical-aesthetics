## §3 document body (XL)

**⚠OWNER PLACEHOLDER, FOR COUNSEL.** The body ships as the clause headings above with counsel's text
underneath. Two things are FIXED by upstream decisions and must survive whatever counsel returns.

**(a) Not one word of the chiropractic template survives.**
The retiring property's disclaimer was adapted from a chiropractic template. The phrase
"chiropractic healthcare advice" and every sentence built around it is DELETED, not edited. This is
a deletion instruction, not a rewrite instruction: a medical aesthetics practice must not carry
another discipline's disclaimer language.

**(b) The results language is the practice's own honest ceiling.** These two sentences are the
substance and must appear in the finished document:

> Results are not guaranteed and may vary from person to person.

> An evaluation determines whether a treatment is appropriate at all, which is a decision about
> suitability rather than a safety guarantee.

**⚠BANNED IN THIS DOCUMENT AS EVERYWHERE:** outcome guarantees; `board certified` in any phrasing;
any wording implying a dermatology board specialty or the word "dermatologist"; unqualified
absolutes ("zero downtime", "painless", "no risk", "works on any skin type"); and any superiority or
definite-article uniqueness construction.

**⚠OWNER, coupled to F4 (promotions):** the `$500 OFF` promotion published on a retiring property has
restrictions that live on a SEPARATE disclaimer page. It either retires with the domain, or it
republishes with its terms adjacent and legible. It is not lifted onto this site without them.
