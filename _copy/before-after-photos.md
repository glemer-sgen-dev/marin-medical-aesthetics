## §2 the standard every published pair has to meet (M)

**Section heading:** What a published pair has to satisfy

Every before and after pair that ever appears here has to meet all of the following. This is the
permanent standard, not a temporary position while the page is empty:

- **First-party only.** The photographs are of actual patients of this practice, treated at this
  practice. Nothing from a device manufacturer, a stock library or another clinic.
- **Comparable conditions.** The same view, the same pose, and the same lighting on both sides. A
  pair that flatters through a change of angle or light is not evidence of anything.
- **A documented release on file** for each patient, for each pair.
- **Otherwise, no image.** If any of the above is missing, the pair does not publish. There is no
  version of this page that fills the space with something approximate.

---

## §3 what the law requires alongside each pair (S)

**Section heading:** What appears next to every pair

Two statements are required by California law and are rendered next to any pair, legibly, rather
than buried in a footer:

- **A model disclosure**, wherever the person shown is anyone other than an actual patient of this
  practice who underwent the advertised procedure.
- **"The same results may not occur for all patients."**

These are a legal control, not a stylistic preference. Under California Business and Professions Code
section 651, a violation is a misdemeanour and is independently good cause to suspend a physician's
licence.
