## §1 hero (S)

**H1:** Clear V in San Anselmo

Laser treatment directed at visible vessels: broken capillaries, facial veins and small vascular

---

## §2 the problem it is aimed at (M)

**Section heading:** What people come in with

Fine red or purple vessels around the nose and cheeks that concealer blurs without hiding, and small
vascular marks that have been there long enough to feel permanent.

---

## §3 how this practice handles it (M)

**Section heading:** How treatment here is decided

- **The evaluation covers function as well as appearance**, and separates discrete vessels from
  general flushing, which need different treatment.
- **It ends in a written plan you keep.**
- **It decides whether a treatment is appropriate at all**, including whether an underlying condition
  such as rosacea should be addressed alongside.

An evaluation determines whether a treatment is appropriate at all. It is not a safety or outcome
guarantee.

---

## §4 what the treatment involves (S)

**Section heading:** What a session is like

The laser targets the blood within the vessel, which absorbs the energy and closes it. Individual
vessels can respond quickly, sometimes within one session, though more diffuse patterns take a
series.

Sensation is a brief snap. Some redness or slight bruising afterwards is normal.

## §5 who this is generally for, and what is published about results (S)

**Section heading:** Who it suits, and what is claimed

This practice's patients are typically adults roughly 35-65 across Marin County who want visible
improvement in skin, body contour or pelvic-floor function, and who specifically want a physician
rather than a technician assessing whether a treatment suits them at all.

No outcome figures and no before and after photographs are published, because none is on file with a
documented patient release.

---

## §6 what this does not do (S)

**Section heading:** What this is not

- **Not a treatment for the underlying tendency.** It closes the vessels you have; it does not stop
  new ones forming.
- **Not a substitute for vascular assessment** where leg veins are accompanied by aching, swelling or
  heaviness.
- **Not permanent** in the sense of preventing recurrence.
- No outcome guarantee and no "typical results" framing.

## §8 questions people actually ask (M)

**Section heading:** Common questions

**How many sessions?**
Discrete vessels can respond quickly. Diffuse patterns take a series. Which you have is established at
the evaluation.

**Will they come back?**
Treated vessels do not reopen, but new ones can form, particularly with rosacea.

**Are leg veins treated the same way?**
Not identically, and leg veins carry a question about circulation that facial vessels do not. If your
legs ache or swell, say so.

**What does it cost?**
Quote-only pricing. During your consultation, we will build a customized treatment plan with packaged
pricing.
