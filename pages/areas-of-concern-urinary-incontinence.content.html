What it costs

What this actually costs people

It is rarely the leaking itself. It is the planning around it: knowing where the bathrooms are, choosing clothes for it, carrying spares, skipping the exercise class, thinking twice about the trampoline with the grandchildren, drinking less than you should before going out.

Most people wait years to raise it, often because nobody has ever asked. It gets treated as an inevitable consequence of childbirth or age, and it is neither.

What changes

Why this starts with an evaluation

The type matters

Leaking on exertion and leaking with sudden urgency are different problems with different answers. Treating one as the other does not work.

Some causes need referral, not a device

Certain presentations need urological or gynaecological assessment first. An evaluation is where that gets identified, and this practice will say so.

It is a medical assessment, not a booking form

That is the whole reason it happens here in the same appointment as everything else.

An evaluation determines whether a treatment is appropriate at all. It is not a safety or outcome guarantee.

How it is approached

How this is approached here

Where a pelvic-floor strengthening approach is appropriate, this practice offers non-invasive treatment you receive fully clothed and seated. There is no undressing, no catheter, no anaesthetic and no recovery time.

Whether it suits you, and whether something else needs to happen first, is decided at the evaluation.

Common questions

01

Do I have to undress?

No. The treatment discussed here is received fully clothed and seated.

02

Isn't this just what happens after having children?

It is common after childbirth. That does not make it untreatable, and treating it as inevitable is the reason most people wait years before asking.

03

Won't Kegels fix it?

Sometimes, and they are worth doing. When they have not worked, it is usually because the exercise is not reaching the muscle or the problem is a different type of incontinence. That distinction is part of the evaluation.

04

Is this embarrassing to bring up?

It is a medical concern and it is treated as one here. It sits on this site at the same weight as skin and body for exactly that reason.
