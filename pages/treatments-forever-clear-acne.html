Treatments

Forever Clear BBL in San Anselmo

Broadband light directed at active acne, using different wavelengths in sequence within the same session.

Physician-supervised medical aesthetics at Marin Medical Aesthetics, San Anselmo.

Book a consultation Start from the concern

What people come in with

  • Active breakouts that have not settled with topical treatment.
  • Years of cycling through products, usually as an adult.
  • Wariness of oral medication.

The frustration is usually not the acne alone but the pattern: improvement, relapse, and a growing suspicion that nothing will hold.

The shelf that pattern produces. Most people arrive with several years of it.

What changes

How treatment here is decided

  • The evaluation covers function as well as appearance, including medications and hormonal factors, because adult acne is rarely only a surface problem. A licensed MD performs it.
  • It ends in a written plan you keep, including what to stop using.
  • It decides whether a treatment is appropriate at all, and severe acne may be better served by a referral than by light.

An evaluation determines whether a treatment is appropriate at all. It is not a safety or outcome guarantee.

How it is approached

What a session is like

Different wavelengths are applied in sequence within one appointment.

  • Felt as warmth and a brief snap.
  • Skin is usually flushed afterwards, settling within a day for most people.
A prepared tray. The appointment is the short part of this.

It is delivered as a series.

Acne treatment is a course, not an event, and the plan says how many and over what period.

Who this is for

Who it suits, and what is claimed

This practice's patients are typically adults roughly 35-65 across Marin County who want visible improvement in skin, body contour or pelvic-floor function, and who want a physician evaluating whether a treatment is appropriate for them at all.

Adult acne is a common reason people arrive here. No outcome figures and no before and after photographs are published, because none is on file with a documented patient release.

The ceiling

What this is not

These are the shape of the treatment rather than a list of its failures.

  • Not a cure, and not a permanent end to breakouts.
  • Not a treatment for acne scarring. Scarring is a separate problem, usually addressed after the acne is controlled.
  • Not a replacement for medical management where acne is severe or scarring rapidly.
  • No "clear skin" promise and no outcome guarantee.

A promise of permanently clear skin is describing something this treatment does not do.

Starting from the concern replaces shopping a device grid. The treatments index still exists and is still useful; it now sits downstream of the concern rather than ahead of it.

Before you book

Common questions

01

Will this clear my skin?

No such promise is made. It is one part of a plan, and the plan usually includes changes to what you are already using.

02

Can I have it while I am breaking out?

Yes. This one is aimed at active acne, which is what separates it from the treatments used for scarring.

03

How many sessions?

A series. The number and the period are set at the evaluation and written into the plan.

04

What does it cost?
