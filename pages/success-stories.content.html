The standard

What every published testimonial ships under

Published here rather than assumed, so you can hold the practice to it:

  • No fake, insider, incentivised or employee-written review.
  • Nothing solicited in exchange for a discount, a product, or anything else of value.
  • No selective gating. Patients are not screened for likely sentiment before being asked.
  • Negative reviews are not suppressed, and the rating shown is the one the platform reports.

This is the standard the FTC's trade regulation rule on consumer reviews and testimonials (16 C.F.R. Part 465, effective 2024-10-21) sets, and it is the standard this page holds itself to.

In patients' own words

Where a patient's words are ordinary description, the practice mirrors them in its own register: approachable, kind, warm, professional.

Where a patient's words are a claim, they stay in quotation marks with the platform, the reviewer and the rating attached, because they are that person's judgement and not the practice's own assertion.

"THE BEST MED SPA EVER" (Google, Esme S., 5 stars).

That quotation is a customer's, and it is presented as one. It is not a heading, and it is not restated as the practice's own claim about itself.

No written patient stories are published yet, and none has been invented to fill the space. A written story publishes only under a documented release from the patient. Until those exist, this page carries the third-party review corpus above and the standard it is held to.
