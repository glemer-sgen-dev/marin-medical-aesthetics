The part people cover rather than treat

The neck is where people notice the gap between how old they feel and how old they look. It is also routinely left out of a skincare routine that stops at the jawline, and it shows the omission.

Scarves, collars and camera angles are the usual response, for years, before anyone asks whether something can be done.

Usually years later than it needed to be

What changes

Why this starts with an evaluation

Skin, muscle and fat are assessed separately. Vertical banding is muscular, crepey texture is skin, and fullness under the chin is neither. They need different answers.

The ceiling gets named. Non-surgical treatment has a limit here, and where your expectation sits beyond it you will be told, not sold a series that cannot reach it.

This practice does not perform surgery, which removes any incentive to talk you toward or away from it.

An evaluation determines whether a treatment is appropriate at all. It is not a safety or outcome guarantee.

Where the limit is named rather than worked around

How it is approached

How the neck is approached here

Where appropriate, energy-based skin tightening and collagen-stimulating treatment, planned over a series. The neck heals more slowly than the face and is treated more conservatively for that reason, which is a clinical constraint rather than a cautious style.

Adults roughly 35-65 across Marin County who want visible improvement in skin, body contour or pelvic-floor function, and who want a physician evaluating whether a treatment is appropriate for them at all.

Before you book

Common questions

01

Are the vertical bands the same as loose skin?

No. Banding is muscle and loose skin is skin. A plan aimed at one does little for the other, which is why they are separated at the evaluation.

02

Does the neck respond as well as the face?

Generally less well and more slowly. Anyone promising you facial results on the neck is not describing the tissue accurately.

03

How many sessions?

It is a series, and the number is set at the evaluation rather than guessed here.
