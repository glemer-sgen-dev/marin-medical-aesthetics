Local community support in Marin County

The practice supports a number of local organisations, and Dr. Bacchi volunteers with RotaCare. This page lists what that actually consists of.

Why a single-physician practice publishes this

Because it is checkable, and because it is true. A practice this size does not have a corporate giving programme; it has a physician who volunteers at a free clinic and a short list of local organisations it puts something behind. Saying that plainly is worth more than a page of language about community and giving back.

It is also the register the practice actually has. Warm, specific, unshowy. That voice is the one thing in the existing material that is genuinely its own, and it is corroborated by the way patients describe her in their own reviews.

Who the practice supports

RotaCare. Dr. Bacchi volunteers with RotaCare, the free clinic network serving patients without insurance coverage.

Other local organisations are backed as well. They are not listed here yet, because the list has not been confirmed for publication, and naming an organisation that has not confirmed it would be worse than publishing a short one. Each entry, when it lands, names the organisation and, where it is documented, what the support actually consists of.

  • Every organisation named here is one this practice genuinely backs. Nothing is listed for appearances.
  • Nothing on this page is an award, a rating or an affiliation you cannot check. Where support consists of volunteering, it says volunteering. Where it consists of something else, it says what.
  • The physician performs the evaluation and makes the clinical decision. That does not change because a practice is small, and it is the same commitment here as on every other page.

Nothing is written here about what other practices in Marin do or do not do. This page is about this one.

How to check any of this

Every organisation named above appears because there is a real relationship behind it, and each one can be contacted independently. Nothing here is an award, a ranking, a certification or an affiliation that exists only as a mark on a website.

No logo appears on this page. An organisation's mark is its own, and it goes up only with that organisation's permission. Until then a name in plain text is both safer and more honest.
