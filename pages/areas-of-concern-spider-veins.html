Spider veins in San Anselmo

Threadlike red, blue or purple vessels branching close to the surface, on the face or the legs.

Book a consultation Explore Clear V

What it costs

Why they change what you wear

Leg veins in particular tend to decide clothing. People stop wearing shorts, dresses and swimwear long before the veins are extensive, and rarely mention why. They arrive quietly, over years of standing, pregnancies or genetics.

They also tend to be dismissed as purely cosmetic, which means the question of whether something else is going on often never gets asked. Spider veins and larger varicose findings are not the same thing, and they are not handled the same way.

What changes

Why this starts with an evaluation

  • Face and legs are different problems. Facial vessels and leg veins have different causes and different treatments; being told they are the same thing is a sign nobody looked properly.
  • Underlying venous disease has to be considered. Leg veins can indicate circulation problems that call for vascular assessment rather than cosmetic treatment, and a physician evaluation is where that question gets raised.
  • Aching or swelling changes everything. Symptoms move this out of the cosmetic category, and the written plan you keep is sometimes a referral.
A comprehensive health evaluation forms part of any treatment here. On a vascular concern the health history is doing real work, not ceremony.

An evaluation determines whether a treatment is appropriate at all. It is not a safety or outcome guarantee.

How it is approached

How spider veins are approached here

For facial vessels, Clear V is light-based treatment aimed at the vessel itself. For veins on the legs the evaluation comes first, and it may conclude that vascular assessment is the appropriate next step rather than treatment here. That is a genuine possible outcome and it is stated up front.

Whether Clear V suits your veins is the evaluation's decision rather than this page's.

Who tends to come here for this

Adults roughly 35-65 across Marin County who want visible improvement in skin, body contour or pelvic-floor function, and who want a physician evaluating whether a treatment is appropriate for them at all.

The ceiling

What is not claimed here

  • No permanence. New vessels form over time.
  • Results are not guaranteed and vary from patient to patient. No "typical results" framing is used here.
  • No before and after photographs, because none is published with a documented patient release yet.
  • No claim that any treatment addresses underlying venous disease. Where that is suspected, it is referred.
  • Varicose findings are referred out, not treated here. No surgery, no medical or surgical dermatology, no skin-cancer treatment, no laser hair removal, no laser tattoo removal and no CoolSculpting.

Before you book

Common questions

01

Are leg veins and facial veins treated the same way?

No. They differ in cause, depth and treatment, and veins on the legs carry a question about circulation that facial vessels do not.

02

Are mine spider veins or varicose veins?

That distinction is what the appointment is for, and one of the two answers is a referral. It is not something this page should tell you.

03

My legs ache as well. Does that matter?

Yes, and it is worth saying at the appointment. Aching, swelling or heaviness suggests this is not purely cosmetic and may need vascular assessment first.

04

Will they come back?

New vessels can form. This is managed rather than solved permanently, and how often you would return is set out in the written plan rather than promised here.

05

What does it cost?
