Why it is stubborn

The area that ignores everything that works elsewhere

Submental fullness is famously stubborn. People lose weight everywhere else and watch this stay exactly as it was, which is what makes it disproportionately frustrating relative to its size.

It also drives more avoidance of photographs and video calls than almost anything else on this list.

What changes

Why this starts with an evaluation

Fat, skin and muscle are separated. Fullness under the chin can be fat, loose skin, a muscular band, or the position of the jaw itself. They need different responses and one of them needs none of the available treatments.

Jaw and neck posture are considered. Some of what reads as a double chin is structural and will not change with any treatment offered here.

The realistic degree of change is set. Against your anatomy, not a stock photograph.

Where a disqualification is delivered, not discovered

How it is approached

How this is approached here

Where fat is the main component and treatment is appropriate, non-surgical fat-reduction and skin-tightening options are discussed. Where loose skin is the main component, the plan is different and the expected result is different.

Not offered here: liposuction, or any surgical approach. Where that is what your result requires, the evaluation says so.

An evaluation determines whether a treatment is appropriate at all. It is not a safety or outcome guarantee.

Common questions

Will losing weight fix it?

Sometimes, and often not. Submental fat is frequently the last to respond, and for some people it does not respond at all. That is not a failure of effort.

Is it fat or loose skin?

That is the question the evaluation answers, and it changes the plan completely.

03

Is this the same as the branded fat-freezing treatment I have seen advertised?
