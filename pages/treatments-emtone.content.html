What people come in with

Dimpling through the thighs and buttocks, present regardless of body size, alongside skin that has lost some firmness over the same area.

Most people arrive having tried creams, brushes and wraps, which address a mechanism that is not the one producing cellulite.

Most people have a shelf of these before they book.

That is not a failure of effort. It is a mismatch between the product and the structure producing the dimpling.

What changes

How treatment here is decided

  • The evaluation covers function as well as appearance, and separates the tethering structure causing dimpling from laxity in the skin above it. A licensed MD performs that evaluation.
  • It ends in a written plan you keep, with the series length and review point stated.
  • It decides whether a treatment is appropriate at all.

An evaluation determines whether a treatment is appropriate at all. It is not a safety or outcome guarantee.

What a session is like

A handpiece is moved over the treatment area, delivering warmth and pressure together. It is generally described as a warm deep massage. There is no numbing and no recovery period. It is a series, spaced over several weeks.

Who this is for

Who it suits, and what is claimed

This practice's patients are typically adults roughly 35-65 across Marin County who want visible improvement in skin, body contour or pelvic-floor function, and who want a physician evaluating whether a treatment is appropriate for them at all.

No percentage figures, no outcome figures and no before and after photographs are published, because none is this practice's own and verified.

The Medical Board of California requires an appropriate prior examination where prescriptive drugs and devices will be used, and that examination may not be delegated to registered nurses. That is why the evaluation comes first here, stated as the reason the process exists rather than as a safety claim.

The ceiling

What this is not

This changes how the area looks. It does not change what is underneath it.

  • Not an elimination of cellulite. Appearance improves; the underlying structure remains.
  • Not a weight-loss or fat-reduction treatment.
  • Not a treatment for significant loose skin.
  • No outcome guarantee and no "typical results" framing.
