Treatments

Scarlet RF in San Anselmo

Radiofrequency microneedling: fine needles deliver energy below the surface to stimulate collagen and tighten.

Physician-supervised medical aesthetics at Marin Medical Aesthetics, San Anselmo.

What it costs

What people come in with

Three things that tend to arrive together:

  • Early laxity along the jaw and neck.
  • Softening definition.
  • Texture that surface treatments have improved but not resolved.

Often people who feel the change is structural rather than superficial, and who are not ready to consider surgery.

Reception, San Anselmo. Most people raise this long before they are ready to think about surgery.

Being in that position is not indecision. It is the position most of this page is written for.

What changes

How treatment here is decided

  • The evaluation covers function as well as appearance, including how much laxity is actually present and whether energy-based treatment can reach your expectation. A licensed MD performs it.
  • It ends in a written plan you keep, including the number of sessions and the review point.
  • It decides whether a treatment is appropriate at all, and where the answer is that a surgical result is what you are describing, that is said plainly.

An evaluation determines whether a treatment is appropriate at all. It is not a safety or outcome guarantee.

How it is approached

What a session is like

Three stages, and it is the third most people are deciding about:

  • Numbing cream first, and long enough for it to work.
  • Fine needles enter the skin and deliver radiofrequency energy at a set depth, felt as heat and pressure.
  • Skin is red and warm afterwards, usually settling over a day or two, with small marks possible.

None of that is the part that decides the outcome.

Collagen change is slow, so results are assessed months rather than days after the series.

Who tends to come here for this

This practice's patients are typically adults roughly 35-65 across Marin County who want visible improvement in skin, body contour or pelvic-floor function, and who want a physician evaluating whether a treatment is appropriate for them at all.

The Medical Board of California requires an appropriate prior examination where prescriptive drugs and devices will be used, and that examination may not be delegated to registered nurses. That is why the evaluation comes first here, stated as the reason the process exists rather than as a safety claim.

The ceiling

What is not claimed here

This is not a facelift and is not presented as an alternative to one; surgery is not offered here. It is not immediate, because the mechanism is collagen remodelling and that takes months. It is not a no-downtime treatment: redness and small marks are expected and described in advance rather than after.

No outcome figures and no before and after photographs are published, because none is on file with a documented patient release, and no "typical results" framing or outcome guarantee is used anywhere on this site.

Starting from the concern replaces shopping a device grid. The treatments index still exists and is still useful; it now sits downstream of the concern rather than ahead of it.

Before you book

Common questions

01

How is this different from ordinary microneedling?

It adds radiofrequency energy at depth, so it targets laxity as well as texture. Which is appropriate for you depends on what the evaluation finds.

02

When will I see something?

Not immediately. Collagen change is gradual, and the review point is set in the plan so you are not judging it too early.

03

How many sessions?

A series. The number is set at the evaluation.

04

What does it cost?

Quote-only pricing. During your consultation, we will build a customized treatment plan with packaged pricing.
