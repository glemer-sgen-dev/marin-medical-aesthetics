Why the jawline reads as age

Why the jawline reads as age

The jawline is one of the first things read in a photograph and one of the last things people think to treat. It changes gradually through a combination of skin laxity, volume shifting, and the underlying support giving way, which is why no single product addresses it.

What changes

Why this starts with an evaluation

The contributing factors get separated. Skin laxity, volume loss and fat position contribute in different proportions in different faces, and the proportions decide the plan.

The limits of non-surgical treatment are stated. Beyond a certain degree of laxity, a non-surgical approach will not deliver what you are picturing. You will be told where you sit.

Referral is a real answer. This practice does not offer surgery, so where surgery is what the result requires, that is said plainly instead of substituting something else.

An evaluation determines whether a treatment is appropriate at all. It is not a safety or outcome guarantee.

How it is approached

How jowls are approached here

Where a non-surgical approach is appropriate, the plan may involve energy-based skin tightening, collagen stimulation, or treatment aimed at supporting the surrounding structure. Expectations are set against your actual starting point rather than against a photograph of someone else's.
