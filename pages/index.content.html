What the evaluation reaches

What a doctor asks that a menu cannot

  • What is actually causing it. The same appearance can have different causes, and the cause decides what is worth doing.
  • What else is going on. Medications, hormonal factors and skin history change what is appropriate, and none of them is visible in a mirror.
  • Whether anything should happen first. Sometimes the honest answer is a referral, and sometimes it is that no treatment is appropriate at all.

The practice publishes it this way: as part of any treatment, she does a comprehensive health evaluation to minimize side effects and teaches clients how their actions can maximize and sustain the benefits they achieve.

What you get

The appointment, and what you leave with

A physician health evaluation covering function as well as appearance, and a written plan you keep: what was assessed, what is recommended, and in what order.

The Medical Board of California requires an appropriate prior examination where prescriptive drugs and devices will be used, and that examination may not be delegated to registered nurses. That is why the sequence here is the sequence it is, stated as the reason the process exists rather than as a claim about safety.
