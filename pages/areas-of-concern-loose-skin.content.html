Areas of concern

Loose skin in San Anselmo

Skin that has lost its snap and no longer sits close to the structure underneath, on the face or the body.

After the effort

The change that follows doing everything right

Loose skin is often what arrives after a success. Weight loss, a fitness change, pregnancy and recovery all leave skin that no longer fits, and the result can feel like being punished for the effort.

It is also the concern with the widest gap between what is advertised and what non-surgical treatment actually delivers.

Most people arrive here after a success, not a failure

What changes

Why this starts with an evaluation

Degree decides everything. Mild laxity responds reasonably to non-surgical treatment. Significant laxity does not, and no number of sessions changes that.

The honest ceiling gets stated. You will be told where your starting point sits relative to what the available treatments can do.

Cause matters. Skin loose after major weight change behaves differently from skin loosening gradually with age.

An evaluation determines whether a treatment is appropriate at all. It is not a safety or outcome guarantee.

How it is approached

How loose skin is approached here

Where the degree of laxity suits it, energy-based tightening and collagen-stimulating treatment over a planned series. Where it does not, the evaluation says so. Being told that a treatment will not reach your expectation is a legitimate and useful outcome of an appointment.

Who tends to come here for this

Adults roughly 35-65 across Marin County who want visible improvement in skin, body contour or pelvic-floor function, and who want a physician evaluating whether a treatment is appropriate for them at all.

No surgical result. Body-contouring surgery and facelift surgery are not offered here, and nothing here is presented as equivalent to them.

No outcome guarantee, no "typical results" framing.

Before you book

Common questions

01

I lost a lot of weight. Can this be tightened?

Partially, depending on how much skin there is. Significant loose skin after major weight loss is generally a surgical question, and you will be told if that is what you are looking at.

02

How many sessions before I see anything?

It is a series, and collagen change is slow. The timeline is set at the evaluation so you are not judging the result at week two.

03

Will it keep improving after the last session?

Collagen-driven change continues for some months. That is also why the result is assessed at a set point rather than immediately.

04
