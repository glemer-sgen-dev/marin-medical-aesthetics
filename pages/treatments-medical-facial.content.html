What changes

How treatment here is decided

The appointment is assembled rather than selected, which is why there is nothing on this page to choose between.

  • The evaluation covers function as well as appearance, and the facial is assembled from what it found. A licensed MD performs that evaluation.
  • It ends in a written plan you keep, including what to use at home, which usually matters more than the appointment itself.
  • It decides whether a treatment is appropriate at all.
Laid out after the decision, not before it.

That second line is not modesty. It is the reason the plan is written down and handed over.

An evaluation determines whether a treatment is appropriate at all. It is not a safety or outcome guarantee.

How it is approached

What an appointment is like

  • Cleansing, exfoliation and extraction, as appropriate.
  • Actives and finishing steps chosen for your skin on the day. Skin may be slightly pink afterwards and generally settles quickly.
Chosen on the day, for the skin in front of us.

Some presentations are better treated more gently than expected. That is a clinical decision made during the appointment, not a downgrade.

Who tends to come here for this

This practice's patients are typically adults roughly 35-65 across Marin County who want visible improvement in skin, body contour or pelvic-floor function, and who want a physician evaluating whether a treatment is appropriate for them at all.

The Medical Board of California requires an appropriate prior examination where prescriptive drugs and devices will be used, and that examination may not be delegated to registered nurses. That is why the evaluation comes first here, stated as the reason the process exists rather than as a safety claim.

The ceiling

What is not claimed here

This is not a substitute for treatment of an actual condition: acne, rosacea and pigment disorders need a plan, not a facial, and the evaluation will say so. It is not a resurfacing treatment and is not equivalent to laser or needling.

It is not a fixed package, and there is no named tier system here. No outcome figures and no before and after photographs are published, because none is on file with a documented patient release, and no outcome guarantee is offered.

Starting from the concern replaces shopping a device grid. The treatments index still exists and is still useful; it now sits downstream of the concern rather than ahead of it.
