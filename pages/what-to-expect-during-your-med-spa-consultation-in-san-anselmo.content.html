What to expect at your consultation

If you have not booked because you do not know what the appointment involves, this is the whole sequence, written out.

The room the appointment happens in

The reason people put this off

Usually not cost, and usually not nerves about a treatment. It is not knowing what the appointment actually is. There is a widespread and reasonable expectation that a consultation at an aesthetics practice is a sales meeting with a device catalogue, and that saying yes to the appointment is most of the way to saying yes to a treatment.

So people wait, research alone, and arrive months later with a device name they picked themselves.

The sequence, step by step

01

You describe the concern in your own words.

Not a treatment name. What you have noticed, how long it has been there, what changes it, and what you have already tried.

02

Dr. Bacchi performs a physician health evaluation.

This covers your history, your medications and other conditions, and the concern itself. It looks at function as well as appearance, so pelvic-floor strength and continence are asked about in the same appointment as skin and contour. In the practice's own published words:

as part of any treatment, she does a comprehensive health evaluation to minimize side effects and teaches clients how their actions can maximize and sustain the benefits they achieve.

The practice's own published words
03

Your current products and routine are reviewed.

This matters more often than people expect, and the first recommendation is frequently to stop something rather than to start something.

04

You are told what is appropriate, and what is not.

Including, sometimes, that nothing here is, or that another kind of doctor is the right next step.

05

You leave with a written plan.

What was assessed, what is recommended, in what order, and what to expect in between. It is yours to keep, to think about, and to take elsewhere for a second opinion.

What you are committing to

The people who book this appointment are typically adults roughly 35-65 across Marin County who want visible improvement in skin, body contour or pelvic-floor function, and who want a physician evaluating whether a treatment is appropriate for them at all.

Booking the consultation commits you to the consultation. Nothing else.

You are not required to book a treatment on the day, and treatment is not the default outcome of the appointment. An evaluation determines whether a treatment is appropriate at all. It is not a safety or outcome guarantee.

On cost

Quote-only pricing. During your consultation, we will build a customized treatment plan with packaged pricing. No price list is published here because a real figure depends on what the evaluation finds.

What to bring

A list of your current medications and the skincare products you are actually using, plus any history relevant to the concern.
