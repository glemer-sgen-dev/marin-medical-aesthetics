Why nothing moves

Doing everything right and seeing nothing move

Fat distribution is largely inherited. You do not get to choose where your body releases it from, which is why a person can lose weight steadily and watch one area stay exactly as it was.

The common conclusion is that the effort was insufficient. Usually it was not.

Not a question of effort

What changes

Why this starts with an evaluation

The tissue gets identified. What reads as fat can be loose skin, muscle separation, or fluid. Each needs a different response.

Suitability is assessed properly. These treatments are for localised pockets in people close to a stable weight. They are not weight-loss treatments, and using them as one does not work.

Health context is part of it. A physician assessment covers what a body-contouring consultation usually does not.

An evaluation determines whether a treatment is appropriate at all. It is not a safety or outcome guarantee.

Where suitability is decided

How it is approached

How this is approached here

Where appropriate, non-invasive treatments that reduce fat in a targeted area, and in some cases build muscle in the same region, planned as a series. Which of them applies, and whether any does, is the output of the evaluation.

Not offered here: liposuction or any surgical fat removal.

Who tends to come here for this

Adults roughly 35-65 across Marin County who want visible improvement in skin, body contour or pelvic-floor function, and who want a physician evaluating whether a treatment is appropriate for them at all.

Before you book

Common questions

01

Will I lose weight?

No. These treatments target a specific area. They are not a route to weight loss and are not offered as one.

02

Do I need to be at a particular weight first?

Generally these suit people close to a stable weight who have a localised pocket that has not responded. The evaluation is where that is assessed honestly.

03

Is the result permanent?

Treated fat cells do not return, but remaining fat can still enlarge with weight gain. The result depends on what happens afterwards.
