What people come in with

Congestion and dullness, and the common problem of a home routine that is not penetrating far enough to change anything. It is often chosen by people between larger treatments who want the interval to be doing something rather than nothing.

What changes

How treatment here is decided

On a treatment built around applied actives, the question is which ones, and that is a clinical question rather than a shopping one.

  • The evaluation covers function as well as appearance, including which actives suit your skin and which are aggravating it. A licensed MD performs it.
  • It ends in a written plan you keep, covering both the appointment and the home routine.
  • It decides whether a treatment is appropriate at all.
Deliberately unbranded. What goes on your skin is a decision, not a range.

An evaluation determines whether a treatment is appropriate at all. It is not a safety or outcome guarantee.

What an appointment is like

  • Cleansing and extraction.
  • Infusion of actives selected for your skin.

It is comfortable, there is no numbing, and people return to normal activity immediately.

Who this is for

Who it suits, and what is claimed

This practice's patients are typically adults roughly 35-65 across Marin County who want visible improvement in skin, body contour or pelvic-floor function, and who want a physician evaluating whether a treatment is appropriate for them at all.

No outcome figures and no before and after photographs are published, because none is on file with a documented patient release.

The Medical Board of California requires an appropriate prior examination where prescriptive drugs and devices will be used, and that examination may not be delegated to registered nurses. That is why the evaluation comes first here, stated as the reason the process exists rather than as a safety claim.

The ceiling

What this is not

  • Not a resurfacing treatment, and not equivalent to laser or needling.
  • Not a treatment for an active skin condition on its own.
  • Not a product endorsement. Naming the system describes what is used here; it is not a claim about the brand's superiority over any other.
  • No outcome guarantee.
