What people come in with

  • Puffiness and fluid retention.
  • Swelling after a procedure.

It is most often used as part of recovery rather than as a treatment for an appearance concern in its own right.

What changes

How treatment here is decided

  • The evaluation covers function as well as appearance, including whether swelling has a cause that needs investigating rather than massaging.
  • It ends in a written plan you keep.
  • It decides whether a treatment is appropriate at all.

An evaluation determines whether a treatment is appropriate at all. It is not a safety or outcome guarantee.

What a session is like

Light, rhythmic, directional strokes. It is much gentler than a standard massage, deliberately so, and it is generally described as relaxing. There is no recovery period.

Folded linen. The whole of the equipment involved.

It is manual throughout. The gentleness is the technique, not a lighter version of one.

Who it suits, and what is claimed

This practice's patients are typically adults roughly 35-65 across Marin County who want visible improvement in skin, body contour or pelvic-floor function, and who want a physician evaluating whether a treatment is appropriate for them at all.

No outcome figures and no before and after photographs are published, because none is on file with a documented patient release.

The ceiling

What this is not

The claim this category is usually sold on is the first thing refused below.

  • Not a detox. No claim is made about removing toxins from the body, because that is not what this does.
  • Not a weight-loss or fat-reduction treatment.
  • Not a treatment for lymphoedema or any medical condition. Persistent or one-sided swelling needs medical assessment, and the evaluation will say so.
  • No outcome guarantee.
