The concern people arrive not having said out loud

Most people raising this have not raised it anywhere else, including with their own doctor. It gets filed under things that are private, or inevitable, or not serious enough to take up an appointment with.

It is none of those. Changes after childbirth, surgery or menopause are common, they have physical causes, and some of them are treatable.

Reception, San Anselmo. This gets raised in an ordinary appointment, in an ordinary room.

What changes

Why this starts with an evaluation

  • The cause is physical and gets treated as such. Pelvic-floor strength, tissue changes and hormonal changes produce different symptoms and need different responses.
  • Some of this needs a different specialist, and the evaluation will say so rather than offering something adjacent. Pain in particular warrants investigation before anything else.
  • It is a physician appointment. A comprehensive health evaluation forms part of any treatment here, which is the difference between this being assessed and it being sold.

An evaluation determines whether a treatment is appropriate at all. It is not a safety or outcome guarantee.

How it is approached

What the practice can look at

Where weakness in the pelvic floor is what the evaluation finds, and this route suits you, this practice offers non-invasive strengthening you receive fully clothed and seated.

Whether that fits your situation, and whether something else should happen first, is the evaluation's decision rather than this page's.

Who tends to come here for this

Adults roughly 35-65 across Marin County who want visible improvement in skin, body contour or pelvic-floor function, and who want a physician evaluating whether a treatment is appropriate for them at all.

The ceiling

What is not claimed here

  • No outcome guarantee, and no claim about satisfaction, relationships or intimacy. Those are not medical outcomes and are not promised here.
  • No success rate or percentage. None is verified for this practice, so none is published.
  • No before and after imagery of any kind, which in this area would be indefensible.
  • No suggestion that treatment substitutes for investigation where pain or bleeding is present. Those need a doctor first.
  • This is not gynaecology and it is not hormone therapy. No surgery, no medical or surgical dermatology, no skin-cancer treatment, no laser hair removal, no laser tattoo removal and no CoolSculpting.
