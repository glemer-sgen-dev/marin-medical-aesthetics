Treatments

Emsculpt NEO in San Anselmo

Non-invasive body treatment that combines radiofrequency heating with intense muscle contraction in the same session.

Book a consultation Start from the concern

What it costs

What people come in with

A specific area that has stopped responding: abdomen, buttocks, arms or thighs. Usually people who train consistently and have hit a plateau in one region while everything else has progressed.

Frequently also people recovering the abdominal strength they had before pregnancy or surgery.

What changes

How treatment here is decided

  • The evaluation covers function as well as appearance, including whether muscle, fat or loose skin is the actual limiting factor. A comprehensive health evaluation forms part of any treatment here.
  • It ends in a written plan you keep, with the number of sessions and the review point set out.
  • It decides whether a treatment is appropriate at all, including screening for contraindications.

An evaluation determines whether a treatment is appropriate at all. It is not a safety or outcome guarantee.

What a session is like

Applicators are strapped to the treatment area. You feel heat alongside strong involuntary muscle contractions. It is unusual rather than painful, and people generally describe muscle soreness afterwards similar to a hard session at the gym. It is a short series, spaced over a few weeks.

Who this is for

Who it suits, and what is claimed

This practice's patients are typically adults roughly 35-65 across Marin County who want visible improvement in skin, body contour or pelvic-floor function, and who want a physician evaluating whether a treatment is appropriate for them at all.

No percentage figures for muscle gain or fat reduction are published here. This device is marketed on such figures; they are the manufacturer's, not this practice's, and no outcome figure has been verified for this practice. No before and after photographs either, for the same reason.

The ceiling

What this is not

  • Not a weight-loss treatment, and no weight outcome is claimed.
  • Not a replacement for exercise. It supplements training.
  • Not a treatment for loose skin, which is a different problem in the same area.
  • Not suitable for everyone. Certain implanted metal and electronic devices are contraindications.

Common questions

Will I lose weight?
