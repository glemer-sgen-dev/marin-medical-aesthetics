2. Information we collect

One build-side fact is fixed and must survive whatever counsel returns. The collection surface is the consultation and contact request form, and it is the only place personal information enters this site. The fields are: name, email, phone (optional), a free-text description of what you would like help with, and preferred contact method. There is no account system, no checkout, and no other collection point on the site.

5. Service providers and processors

A second build-side fact is fixed here. The existing lead-capture forms are vendor-hosted third-party embeds, so submitted enquiries currently land in that third party's system rather than the practice's. A processor disclosure is therefore required, and the migration decision has to be made before this policy is finalised: whichever processor ends up handling the form is the one this clause has to name. Finalising the policy first would document a data flow that is about to change.

6. Third-party apps and links

The third build-side fact. The loyalty-app download page routes to a third-party app with its own data handling. This clause must either cover that app explicitly or explicitly exclude it. Silence is not an option, because a visitor arrives at it from this site.
