What people come in with

Because EXION is a platform rather than a single treatment, people arrive for very different reasons: skin quality and laxity on the face, contour and firmness on the body, or a combination.

That flexibility is also why choosing it from a website is the wrong way round. The applicator is a decision, and it should follow an assessment rather than precede one.

Reception, San Anselmo. The decision this page is about gets made in here, not on a web page.

What changes

How treatment here is decided

  • The evaluation covers function as well as appearance, and determines which application, if any, is relevant to what you have. A licensed MD performs it.
  • It ends in a written plan you keep, naming the area, the series and the review point.
  • It decides whether a treatment is appropriate at all.

An evaluation determines whether a treatment is appropriate at all. It is not a safety or outcome guarantee.

How it is approached

What a session is like

A handpiece is moved across the treatment area, delivering warmth. Sensation is generally described as a warm massage. There is typically no numbing and no recovery period, though the area may be pink afterwards.

It is a series, and the number depends on the area and the goal.

Scope

What this page covers, and what it does not

This page is the device. The concerns it might be used for each have their own page, and those pages are where the thinking belongs.

  • Firmness on the face is one problem.
  • The neck is another, with different tissue and a different answer.
  • The body is a third, where contour is not the same question as skin quality.

If you arrived here looking for one of those three, the rail further down goes straight to it. Nothing is lost by starting there instead.

Who this is for

Who it suits, and what is claimed

This practice's patients are typically adults roughly 35-65 across Marin County who want visible improvement in skin, body contour or pelvic-floor function, and who want a physician evaluating whether a treatment is appropriate for them at all.

No percentage figures, no outcome figures and no before and after photographs are published, because none is this practice's own and verified.

The Medical Board of California requires an appropriate prior examination where prescriptive drugs and devices will be used, and that examination may not be delegated to registered nurses. That is why the evaluation comes first here, stated as the reason the process exists rather than as a safety claim.

The ceiling

What this is not

  • Not a single treatment with a single result. What it does depends entirely on the applicator and the area.
  • Not a facelift or a body-contouring surgery, and not an alternative to either.
  • Not a weight-loss treatment.
  • No outcome guarantee and no "typical results" framing.
