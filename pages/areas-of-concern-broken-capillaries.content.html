Broken capillaries in San Anselmo

Fine red or purple vessels visible close to the surface, most often around the nose, cheeks and chin.

Why cover-up fails

Why concealer stops working

Individual vessels are sharp-edged and sit under the surface, so foundation blurs them without hiding them. They also tend to multiply slowly, so the amount of covering required creeps upward over years.

They frequently sit alongside general flushing, which is why people describe the whole area as "redness" without distinguishing the two.

What changes

Why this starts with an evaluation

Vessels and background redness are separated. They respond to different things, and a plan addressing only one leaves the other visibly behind.

The underlying condition gets identified. Visible vessels are commonly part of rosacea, which changes the plan and the aftercare.

Recurrence is planned for. Treated vessels close; the tendency that produced them does not disappear.

An evaluation determines whether a treatment is appropriate at all. It is not a safety or outcome guarantee.

Said at the start rather than discovered later

How it is approached

How visible vessels are approached here

Where appropriate, light-based treatment aimed specifically at vessels, such as Clear V, which targets the blood within them rather than the surrounding skin. Where general flushing is also present, that is addressed as its own part of the plan, which may involve ClearSilk.

Who this is for

Who tends to come here for this

Adults roughly 35-65 across Marin County who want visible improvement in skin, body contour or pelvic-floor function, and who want a physician evaluating whether a treatment is appropriate for them at all.

Before you book

Common questions

01

Will they come back?

The treated vessels do not reopen, but new ones can form, especially with rosacea. Maintenance is discussed as part of the plan rather than presented later as a surprise.

02

Is this the same as my general redness?

Usually not, and that is the most useful distinction on this page. Discrete vessels and diffuse flushing are separate targets.

03

Does it hurt?

It is usually described as a brief snap. You are told what to expect beforehand.
