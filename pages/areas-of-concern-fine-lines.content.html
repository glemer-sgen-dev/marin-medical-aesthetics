Where it goes wrong

The stage where it is easy to overdo it

Fine lines are the point at which most people first consider treatment, and the point at which the wrong amount of it is most obvious. Too much, too early, on a face that mostly needs skin quality rather than muscle relaxation, is the result everyone is trying to avoid.

What changes

Why this starts with an evaluation

Movement lines and static lines are distinguished. Lines that appear only on expression respond differently from lines etched into the skin at rest.

Skin quality is assessed separately from muscle. Many early lines are a collagen and texture problem wearing a muscle problem's clothing.

Restraint is a plan, not a lack of one. The recommendation may be less than you expected, and that is a clinical position rather than a soft sell.

An evaluation determines whether a treatment is appropriate at all. It is not a safety or outcome guarantee.

How it is approached

How fine lines are approached here

Depending on which type is present, the plan may involve treatment aimed at expression lines such as Botox or Dysport or Emface, collagen-stimulating or resurfacing work where the skin itself is the issue such as ClearSilk or Scarlet RF, or both in sequence. Which applies to you is the output of the evaluation.

Who this is for

Who tends to come here for this

Adults roughly 35-65 across Marin County who want visible improvement in skin, body contour or pelvic-floor function, and who want a physician evaluating whether a treatment is appropriate for them at all.
