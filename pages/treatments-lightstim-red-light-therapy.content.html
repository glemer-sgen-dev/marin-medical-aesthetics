What people come in with

Usually not a standalone concern. It is most often added to support recovery after a more substantial treatment, or used by people who want a gentle, cumulative option with no recovery at all.

Light on a wall. This treatment is light, and that is the whole of what it is.

What changes

How treatment here is decided

  • The evaluation covers function as well as appearance, and this is positioned as a support treatment rather than a primary answer.
  • It ends in a written plan you keep.
  • It decides whether a treatment is appropriate at all, including whether something more substantial is the honest recommendation.

An evaluation determines whether a treatment is appropriate at all. It is not a safety or outcome guarantee.

What a session is like

You sit under the light panel for a set period. It is warm and comfortable, there is no recovery, and you can carry on with your day immediately. Any benefit is cumulative, so it is used repeatedly rather than as a single event.

Who this is for

Who it suits, and what is claimed

This practice's patients are typically adults roughly 35-65 across Marin County who want visible improvement in skin, body contour or pelvic-floor function, and who want a physician evaluating whether a treatment is appropriate for them at all.

No outcome figures and no before and after photographs are published, because none is on file with a documented patient release.

The ceiling

What this is not

  • Not a treatment for a significant concern on its own. It supports; it does not resolve.
  • Not a substitute for laser, needling or injectable treatment where those are what is indicated.
  • Not a medical treatment for any condition, and no therapeutic claim beyond skin quality and post-treatment comfort is made here.
  • No outcome guarantee.
