What a session is like

Pads are placed on the forehead and cheeks. You feel warmth and rhythmic muscle contraction. There are no needles and no numbing, and people generally return to normal activity straight afterwards. It is a short series, typically spaced over a few weeks, with change developing afterwards rather than during.

Who this is for

Who it suits, and what is claimed

This practice's patients are typically adults roughly 35-65 across Marin County who want visible improvement in skin, body contour or pelvic-floor function, and who want a physician evaluating whether a treatment is appropriate for them at all.

No outcome figures and no before and after photographs are published, because none is on file with a documented patient release. This device category is marketed heavily on percentage figures; none is this practice's own, so none appears here.

The Medical Board of California requires an appropriate prior examination where prescriptive drugs and devices will be used, and that examination may not be delegated to registered nurses. That is why the evaluation comes first here, stated as the reason the process exists rather than as a safety claim.

What this is not

This practice offers injectables as well, which is exactly why the second line below is worth reading.

  • Not a facelift, and not an alternative to one. Surgery is not offered here.
  • Not a replacement for injectables, though it is sometimes chosen instead of them. Which suits you is the evaluation's decision, not a marketing position.
  • Not suitable for everyone. Certain implanted electronic and metal devices are contraindications.
  • No outcome guarantee and no percentage claims.
