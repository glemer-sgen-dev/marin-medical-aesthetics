The plateau

The plateau

Progress in a specific area often stalls while everything else continues to improve. The training is consistent, the diet is unchanged, and one region simply refuses to follow.

That plateau is usually a recruitment and volume problem rather than a motivation problem, which is not how it feels at the time.
