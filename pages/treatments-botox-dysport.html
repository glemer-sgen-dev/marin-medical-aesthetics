Botox and Dysport in San Anselmo

Injectable treatment for lines produced by muscle movement, planned and performed by a physician.

Physician-supervised medical aesthetics at Marin Medical Aesthetics, San Anselmo.

What it costs

What people come in with

Lines across the forehead, between the brows and around the eyes that deepen on expression. Often a sense of looking tired or cross when neither is true.

Many people arrive having had treatment elsewhere that was either too much, too little, or placed without much thought about how their face actually moves.

The part that decides the outcome is the planning, and it happens before anything on this tray is opened.

How treatment here is decided

  • The evaluation covers function as well as appearance, and it happens before any product is discussed as suitable for you. A licensed MD performs that evaluation.
  • It ends in a written plan you keep, including dose, placement and interval.
  • It decides whether a treatment is appropriate at all. Sometimes the honest recommendation is less than you came in expecting, or none.

An evaluation determines whether a treatment is appropriate at all. It is not a safety or outcome guarantee.

How it is approached

What an appointment is like

A series of small injections into specific muscles, taking a few minutes. Effect develops over several days rather than immediately, and it wears off gradually, which is why interval matters as much as dose.

Placement is decided from how your face moves, not from a diagram.

Which of those decisions applies to you is settled at the appointment rather than on this page.

Who it suits, and what is claimed

This practice's patients are typically adults roughly 35-65 across Marin County who want visible improvement in skin, body contour or pelvic-floor function, and who want a physician evaluating whether a treatment is appropriate for them at all.

No before and after photographs are published, because none is on file with a documented patient release. No outcome figures are published either.

The Medical Board of California requires an appropriate prior examination where prescriptive drugs and devices will be used, and that examination may not be delegated to registered nurses. That is why the evaluation comes first here, stated as the reason the process exists rather than as a safety claim.

The ceiling

What this is not

  • Not a treatment for static lines. Lines etched in at rest soften at best; they need something additional.
  • Not a filler. It relaxes muscle rather than restoring volume, and the two are frequently confused.
  • Not permanent, and not a substitute for skin quality work.
  • Not made safe by a credential. Harm has occurred with both licensed and unlicensed injectors, so no claim of that kind is made here.

Before you book

Common questions

01

Will I look frozen?

That result comes from dose and placement, which are clinical decisions. The recommendation here is frequently more conservative than people expect.

02

How long does it last?

Months rather than weeks, and it varies by person and by area. Your expected interval is part of the written plan.

03

Does it hurt?

Brief and minor for most people. It is not described as painless, because that would be an unqualified absolute rather than a description.

04

What if I do not like it?

Effect is temporary and wears off. That is discussed honestly before you agree to anything, including what can and cannot be adjusted in the meantime.

05

What does it cost?

Quote-only pricing. During your consultation, we will build a customized treatment plan with packaged pricing.

None of these is a promise about your own result. They describe how the appointment is run.
