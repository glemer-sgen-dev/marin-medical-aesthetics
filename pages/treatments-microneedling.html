Treatments

Microneedling in San Anselmo

Controlled micro-injury that prompts the skin to rebuild collagen, aimed at texture and scarring.

Physician-supervised medical aesthetics at Marin Medical Aesthetics, San Anselmo.

Book a consultation Start from the concern

What it costs

What people come in with

Acne scarring, enlarged pores, and a surface that no longer reflects light evenly. Often people who have improved tone with other treatments and found that texture did not follow.

It is also one of the most common at-home treatments, usually with a device that does not reach the depth required, which is worth knowing before assuming it does not work.

Most people arrive with a home routine already in place.

Nothing about that is wrong. It is a question of how deep the device reaches, which is not something a label tells you.

What changes

How treatment here is decided

On this treatment more than most, order matters: done in the wrong sequence it makes things worse rather than slower.

  • The evaluation covers function as well as appearance, including whether active acne needs settling before scarring is treated. A licensed MD performs it.
  • It ends in a written plan you keep, including the number of sessions and their spacing.
  • It decides whether a treatment is appropriate at all.

That first line is the reason this page exists in the concern layer rather than as a menu item.

An evaluation determines whether a treatment is appropriate at all. It is not a safety or outcome guarantee.

How it is approached

What a session is like

Before

Numbing cream first, and long enough for it to work.

During

Fine needles create controlled micro-channels at a chosen depth. The depth is the decision, and it is made for your skin rather than set by the device.

Afterwards

Skin is red and warm, similar to sunburn, usually settling within a day or two.

It is a series, spaced to allow collagen to respond between sessions.

Who this is for

Who it suits, and what is claimed

This practice's patients are typically adults roughly 35-65 across Marin County who want visible improvement in skin, body contour or pelvic-floor function, and who want a physician evaluating whether a treatment is appropriate for them at all.

No outcome figures and no before and after photographs are published, because none is on file with a documented patient release.

The Medical Board of California requires an appropriate prior examination where prescriptive drugs and devices will be used, and that examination may not be delegated to registered nurses. That is why the evaluation comes first here, stated as the reason the process exists rather than as a safety claim.

The ceiling

What this is not

  • Not a treatment for active acne. Needling through active breakouts can spread inflammation, so acne is settled first.
  • Not scar removal. Scarring is improved in appearance, not erased.
  • Not the same as an at-home roller, which works at a fraction of the depth.
  • No outcome guarantee and no "typical results" framing.

Starting from the concern replaces shopping a device grid. The treatments index still exists and is still useful; it now sits downstream of the concern rather than ahead of it.

Before you book

Common questions

01

Is this the same as my at-home roller?

No. Depth is the variable that matters, and home devices work well above the level at which collagen remodelling happens.

02

Can it be done while I am breaking out?

Generally not. Active acne is settled first, because needling through it risks spreading inflammation.

03

Will my scars go?

They improve in appearance. Removal is not a claim made here.

04

How many sessions?

A series, spaced apart. The number is set at the evaluation.

05

What does it cost?

Quote-only pricing. During your consultation, we will build a customized treatment plan with packaged pricing.
