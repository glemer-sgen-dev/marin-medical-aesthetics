What actually changes after pregnancy

Several things at once, which is why single treatments so often disappoint. Abdominal tone that has not returned. Skin that no longer sits as it did. Fat that has redistributed. And, very commonly and much less often mentioned, a pelvic floor that no longer holds.

That last one is why this page exists in its current form. Most plans marketed under this heading address only what is visible, and leave the part that affects a person's day untouched.

Daylight in the practice, San Anselmo. What a plan contains is decided after the evaluation, not before you arrive.

How the plan is built

The evaluation covers function as well as appearance

Continence and pelvic-floor strength are assessed in the same appointment as tone, skin and contour, not treated as a separate specialty. A comprehensive health evaluation forms part of any treatment here.

It ends in a written plan you keep

Sequenced across the areas being addressed, so you can see what happens in what order rather than being told as you go.

It decides whether a treatment is appropriate at all

Including how long after delivery it is reasonable to start, and whether anything should be referred first.

An evaluation determines whether a treatment is appropriate at all. It is not a safety or outcome guarantee.

How it is approached

What the plan may cover

Depending entirely on what the evaluation finds, it may combine:

  • Muscle and tone work for the abdomen.
  • Fat and contour treatment where a localised area has not responded.
  • Skin quality and firmness where laxity is present.
  • Pelvic-floor strengthening, received fully clothed and seated, where continence or strength is part of the picture.

It is a plan, not a package. There is no fixed bundle, no tier, and no named tiers on this site.

Who this is for

Who it suits, and what is claimed

This practice's patients are typically adults roughly 35-65 across Marin County who want visible improvement in skin, body contour or pelvic-floor function, and who want a physician evaluating whether a treatment is appropriate for them at all.

No outcome figures, no percentages and no before and after photographs are published, because none is this practice's own and verified.

The ceiling

What this page does not offer or claim

  • Not surgery, and not an alternative to it. Abdominoplasty, liposuction and breast surgery are not offered here. Where a surgical result is what you are describing, the evaluation says so and refers you, rather than assembling an approximation.
  • No "snap back" framing, and no language about your body owing anyone a recovery.
  • No "no downtime" or "no side effects" claim. Both are unqualified absolutes and neither survives here.
  • No timeline promise relative to delivery, feeding or anything else.
  • No outcome guarantee. No medical or surgical dermatology, no skin-cancer treatment, no laser hair removal, no laser tattoo removal and no CoolSculpting.

Before you book

Common questions

01

How long after having a baby can I start?

That is an evaluation question and depends on delivery, recovery and whether you are feeding. No timeline is published here because it is not a general answer.

02

Is this the same as surgery?

No. It is explicitly non-surgical, which is why the qualifier is in the name. If what you want needs surgery, you will be told.

03

Does it include the pelvic floor?

It can, and that is much of the point. It is assessed in the same appointment rather than treated as somebody else's department.

04

Is it a set package?

No. There is no fixed bundle. The plan is built from what the evaluation finds.

05

What does it cost?
