How it is approached

What a session is like

Pulses of light are applied across the treatment area, felt as a brief snap.

  • Treated brown spots typically darken over the following days before flaking away.
  • Redness settles gradually rather than at once.
Light across a wall. The treatment is light too, which is why it addresses colour rather than texture.

It is delivered as a series, with maintenance discussed as part of the plan rather than raised later.

Who tends to come here for this

This practice's patients are typically adults roughly 35-65 across Marin County who want visible improvement in skin, body contour or pelvic-floor function, and who want a physician evaluating whether a treatment is appropriate for them at all.

The Medical Board of California requires an appropriate prior examination where prescriptive drugs and devices will be used, and that examination may not be delegated to registered nurses. That is why the evaluation comes first here, stated as the reason the process exists rather than as a safety claim.

The ceiling

What is not claimed here

This is not a resurfacing treatment: it addresses colour more than texture. It is not suitable for every skin type, because light-based pigment treatment carries real risk in deeper skin tones, and that is a physician assessment rather than a setting on a device. It is not a screening service either: suspicious lesions are referred, and skin-cancer treatment is not offered here.

It is also not permanent. New sun-driven pigment forms with further exposure, which is why sun protection is written into the plan rather than mentioned in passing. No outcome figures and no before and after photographs are published, because none is on file with a documented patient release.

Starting from the concern replaces shopping a device grid. The treatments index still exists and is still useful; it now sits downstream of the concern rather than ahead of it.

Before you book

Common questions

01

Why do the spots get darker first?

That is the expected response when pigment is targeted, and it typically resolves over days. It is explained before treatment, not after.

02

Can my hands and chest be treated?

Often, yes, and they are among the more common requests.

03

Is it safe for my skin tone?

That is assessed rather than assumed. No treatment here is described as suitable for every skin type.

04

Will it stop new spots forming?
