Areas of concern

Sun damage in San Anselmo

The cumulative record of years outdoors: uneven tone, brown patches, roughness, and a surface that no longer reflects light evenly.

Why it feels sudden

Why it seems to arrive all at once

Sun damage accumulates invisibly for decades and then becomes visible over a short period, which is why it feels sudden. Much of it was acquired long before anyone thought about it, and in Marin the exposure is year-round rather than seasonal.

It is also the concern most often self-treated with brightening products that address the top layer of a problem sitting several layers down.

Year-round exposure, not seasonal

What changes

Why this starts with an evaluation

Not every brown mark is sun damage. Some are melasma, which worsens under treatments that suit sun damage. Some need looking at for other reasons entirely.

Anything suspicious gets named. A lesion that should be assessed for skin cancer is referred, not treated cosmetically. This practice does not offer skin-cancer treatment and does not pretend otherwise.

Depth decides the approach. Surface pigment and deeper textural change are different problems and are sequenced accordingly.

An evaluation determines whether a treatment is appropriate at all. It is not a safety or outcome guarantee.

How it is approached

How sun damage is approached here

Depending on what the evaluation finds, the plan may involve broadband light aimed at pigment and redness such as Forever Young BBL, resurfacing where texture as well as tone has changed such as Halo laser or ClearSilk, and a daily protection plan without which any of it undoes itself.

Who this is for

Who tends to come here for this

Adults roughly 35-65 across Marin County who want visible improvement in skin, body contour or pelvic-floor function, and who want a physician evaluating whether a treatment is appropriate for them at all.

Before you book

Common questions

01

Can the damage be reversed?

Appearance improves. The history does not reverse, and a practice claiming otherwise is selling something.

02

Should I get a mark checked first?

If anything has changed in size, shape or colour, yes, and by someone who treats skin cancer. That is not offered here, and the evaluation will tell you plainly if that is what it sees.

03

Will it come back?

Pigment returns with further exposure. Daily protection is the part of the plan that decides whether the result holds.
