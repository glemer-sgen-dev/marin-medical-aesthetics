5  Signature motif: the ruled record
01Concernwhat brought you in
02Physician evaluationfunction and appearance
03Written planyours to keep

The motif is the ruled record: a numbered, hairline-separated row set that can carry a process, a service list or a set of facts. It is the identity's one repeatable structural idea, deliberately austere, because the differentiator here is rigour. Circle-masked imagery (blueprint H2) is declined in this concept: soft masks pull the practice toward the spa register it is positioned against.
