Areas of concern in San Anselmo

Start here, with what you have actually noticed. This page is the front door of the site, and it comes before the treatment index on purpose: naming the concern is the first step, and choosing a device is a later one that belongs to the physician.

Concern first, then evaluation, then a plan

Step oneYou name itYou name what is bothering you.
Step twoA physician evaluates itA physician evaluates what is actually going on and decides what is appropriate.
Step threeYou leave with a planThe output is a written plan you keep.

That order is the whole architecture of this practice, and it is why the concern list, not the device list, is the thing you meet first.
