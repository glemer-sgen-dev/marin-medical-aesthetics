Book a consultation in San Anselmo

What the appointment covers

A comprehensive health evaluation, performed by a physician, before any treatment is discussed as suitable for you.

That means the appointment covers your history, your medications, the condition in front of her, and how the two interact. It is the part this practice is organised around.

as part of any treatment, she does a comprehensive health evaluation to minimize side effects and teaches clients how their actions can maximize and sustain the benefits they achieve.

The written plan

You leave with a plan in writing. It is yours to keep, to think about, and to take elsewhere for a second opinion if you want one. A plan you cannot take away is not a plan; it is a quote.

It says what was assessed, what is recommended, in what order, and what to expect between appointments. It also says what to stop doing where that matters.

What it costs

Quote-only pricing. During your consultation, we will build a customized treatment plan with packaged pricing.

No price list is published on this site, and no "from" figure either, because a real figure depends on what the evaluation finds. You will be told the cost of your plan as part of receiving it, before you commit to anything.

Before you book

Do I need to know what treatment I want?

No. Arriving with a concern rather than a device name is the better starting point, and it is how this site is organised.

Will I be treated on the day?

Not necessarily, and not as a default. The evaluation comes first. Where a treatment is appropriate and you want to proceed, that is discussed as part of the plan.

What if nothing is appropriate?

Then you will be told that. It is a real outcome of an evaluation and not a failed appointment.

Who performs the evaluation?

Dr. Chris Bacchi, MD. She is the physician making the clinical decision.

What should I bring?

A list of your current medications and skincare products, and any history relevant to the concern. The products matter more often than people expect.
