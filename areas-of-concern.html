Twenty-one concerns, four groups

Find the word for it

Skin

Texture, pigment, redness, and the changes that follow sun and time.

Acne and acne scarsActive breakouts, and the texture they leave behind once they settle. Age spotsDiscrete darker marks, usually where the sun reached most often. HyperpigmentationPatches of deeper colour that sit unevenly against the surrounding skin. MelasmaA patterned, often symmetrical pigment change that behaves differently from sun spots. Sun damageThe cumulative record of years outdoors, in tone and in texture. Uneven skin textureRoughness, enlarged pores, and a surface that no longer reflects light evenly. RosaceaPersistent flushing and redness that comes and goes on its own schedule. Broken capillariesFine visible vessels, most often around the nose and cheeks. Spider veinsThreadlike vessels close to the surface, on the face or the legs.

Face and structure

Volume, contour, and the way the face carries expression.

Fine linesThe earliest lines, usually where the face moves most. WrinklesLines that stay visible when the face is at rest. JowlsSoftening along the jawline where the edge used to be definite. Turkey neckLoosening and banding through the front of the neck. Double chinFullness under the chin that does not track with overall weight. Loose skinSkin that has lost its snap and no longer sits close to the structure beneath it.

Body

Shape, tone, and the areas that do not respond to effort alone.

CelluliteDimpling through the thigh and buttock, independent of body size. Stubborn fatPockets that stay put through diet and training. Core strengthAbdominal tone that exercise alone has not restored. Muscle developmentDefinition you are working for and not seeing.

Function

Some of what changes is not visible at all. Treated here in the same register, at the same weight, as everything above.

Urinary incontinenceLeaking with a cough, a laugh, a lift, or on the way to the bathroom. Sexual function enhancementChanges in comfort, sensation, or pelvic-floor strength.

On the function group

Listed here plainly and without euphemism.

They are among the most common reasons people book and among the least often named out loud, which is why they sit in the index rather than behind a euphemism.

How this works

Pick the concern that fits. It routes to a physician evaluation, and the evaluation produces the plan. If you already know the device name and would rather start there, the treatment index is the other way in.
